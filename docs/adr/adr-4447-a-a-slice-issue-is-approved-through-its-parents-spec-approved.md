# ADR-4447-A: A slice issue is approved through its parent's spec:approved

A spec approved on a parent issue is built as numbered slices, each with its own issue, and a slice carries neither `spec:approved` nor, honestly, a lightweight marker. `validate-discovery.sh` therefore reads the parent: a slice declares one `Slice N of #M` line before `<!-- spec:start -->` (or at the top of a body without spec markers), and when it has `status:implementing` and no `spec:approved` of its own, the script fetches #M with `gh api` and passes only if #M is open and carries `spec:approved`. It fails closed on a fetch error, a closed or missing parent, a self-reference, a marker that is not `state=complete`, or more than one parent line. `open-pr.sh` accepts that result and records the gate as `parent-approved #M`. The slice's marker is its honest lane (`heavyweight` or `standard`). The lightweight bypass is unchanged and stays for bounded work only. The member-only gate is not weakened: `spec:approved` is still applied by a human on the parent, and the slice only points at it. We rejected letting a slice write its own lightweight marker, which is what two agents did on #4441 and #4442: the work was authorized, but the marker is the one self-applied bypass and "lightweight" was false for a cross-system slice. We rejected requiring a member to label every slice, because the approval already covers the slices of the spec.
