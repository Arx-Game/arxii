# ADR-4341: Patron-scoped favor modifiers are resolved when read

A favor modifier may target "the character's patron" instead of a named being: each favor category (`favor_invocation`, `favor_earning`) has one patron target (`ModifierTarget.targets_patron`, which names no being), and `favor_modifier_total` adds it, through `patron_modifier_total`, only for a being the character is the Chosen of (`chosen_patronage_of`: a patronage whose valence is set and is not PACT). The Chosen invocation ease and earning bonus are the effects of one designated Chosen distinction (`ChosenFavorConfig.chosen_distinction`) on those two targets, granted at character creation and, when staff change the designation, to every Chosen who lacks it. This amends ADR-4333-A, where the Chosen ease and bonus were two `ChosenFavorConfig` numbers applied to any active patronage. It ships with being pacts because a pact sets a PACT patronage, and every Chosen reader that checked "any active patronage" would have handed a pact holder the Chosen bonuses. We rejected a distinction effect per being (one Chosen distinction per god, authored and kept in step for every being with a Chosen), because one distinction resolved against her own patron serves every Chosen; a valence check inside each reader (earning rate, invocation difficulty, the pledge and admin displays), because the rule would be repeated at every site and the next reader would miss it; and keeping the `ChosenFavorConfig` fields, because the modifier system already carries any source against favor targets and #4349 ruled the Chosen ease becomes a favor modifier. Anima recovery stays a `ChosenFavorConfig` threshold, read only for Chosen patronages (`best_patronage_favor`).
