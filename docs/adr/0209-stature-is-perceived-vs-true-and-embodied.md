# ADR-0209: Stature is perceived-vs-true, embodied in people, one hop wide

**Status:** Accepted (#3091, 2026-08-10)

A landed org's deterrence ("Stature") is two numbers, not one: a **true**
strength recomputed weekly (renown of the people who stand with the house +
military + economic + one-hop allied net − open-threat drag) and a
**perceived** strength that converges toward truth with lag and jumps only on
public events (deaths, pact changes, surfaced crises) or spycraft (whisper
campaigns, capped below truth). Predation targets *perceived* weakness. The
renown component is **embodied**: it is carried by specific living people —
members, the head's Court, kin, and union partners (marriage counts both
spouses fully to both houses; consorts count half, senior's side only, gated
on a landed title) — so a death instantly reshapes the map, which is the
marriage-stakes loop the system exists to create. Allied contribution reads
the counterpart's **net** own strength at one hop in both directions (their
crises drag you), never transitively — a superpower's ally's ally must not
deter bandits, and transitive chains would make every pact global. Rejected:
a single public scoreboard (kills the spy game and the Inferna
conceal-your-weakness story); per-observer perception rows (combinatorial
state for depth the covert-window + intel machinery already provides);
transitive ally chains (snowball topology).
