# ADR-5241: A feature takes a quality directly, and the first ten points of qualities are free

ADR-0287 made a physical feature distinctive for one CG point, "Make It Distinctive", and only then put Alluring, Menacing and Majestic up for sale on it at two CG points a tier. Two things went wrong with that. The qualities sat behind a row whose name did not say they were there, so the owner, looking at the Appearance stage on production, could not find them at all ("whatever happened to the distinctive options for different physical features? ... I don't see that reflected at all"). And they were paid from the same hundred CG points as a character's whole life, so a quality on a scar competed with a career.

The owner ruled on 2026-11-21: "I would just give someone 10 free points of making any features have bonuses". Asked what a point buys, the owner chose one tier with no unlock step; asked whether CG points can buy more once the ten are spent, the answer was yes, at the same price.

So a feature takes a quality directly. There is no pick before the three qualities, and the two flags that expressed one (`Distinction.opens_feature`, `requires_feature_opened`) are removed; no row on production set either. A feature is **distinctive** while it holds at least one tier of any quality (`offers.opened_features`), and that is what opens its description and widens its palette to every option the trait carries, as the one-point pick used to. Taking the last tier off closes both again. The first `FEATURE_FREE_POINTS` (10) that a draft's qualities cost are free: each quality keeps its own priced line in the CG point breakdown, and one line, "Free feature points", gives the free part back (`CharacterDraft._append_feature_allowance`). Past ten, a tier costs CG points at the quality's own `cost_per_rank`, which staff author at 1. Unspent free points are lost, and the stage does not ask for them to be spent. The rest of ADR-0287 stands: a distinction is held once per feature, a tier adds +2 to its axis, a quality stops at three tiers in character creation and five in play, and a bonus travels with the feature's visibility.

*Rejected:* keeping the prices as built and only changing the purse (one free point to make a feature distinctive, two a tier). The owner chose against it, and the extra step is what hid the qualities. *Rejected:* a second budget row in the database for the allowance. It is one number the owner set in conversation, the same shape as `STAT_CREATION_BUDGET`; a constant with the ruling quoted beside it is enough until staff need to tune it. *Rejected:* making the first ten points free by pricing each of the first ten tiers at zero. Which tier is "the eleventh" depends on the order of the picks, so a pick's price would change when another pick was removed; one allowance line keeps every pick's own price fixed. *Rejected:* opening the unnatural colours to every feature for nothing. ADR-0287's reason holds: an off-species colour is one of the things that makes a feature distinctive, so it stays tied to the feature being so.
