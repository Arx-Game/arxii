# ADR-5026-B: Armor stands only against the damage types it covers

On every hit in the game, player and NPC, Strike and technique, worn armor stops Physical damage and only the other damage types its item template lists in "Also protects against" (owner, 2026-11-09: "Most armor would only defend against physical, but there could be armor that would defend against other types"). Before this, soak predated damage types and stopped every type alike (ADR-4742-A made untyped damage Physical), so a fire enchantment met plate the same as steel. Covenant protection (the resonant pool: role, facets, mantles, motif, covenant level) stands against every type. An ordinary NPC's single soak number stops Physical only, and its other protections are resistances, which already exist per type. A boss's defenses must be broken, so an opponent with a wall (Boss or Hero Killer tier, or any opponent with a probing threshold) keeps its soak against every type; a fire or shadow part meets the full wall like steel, and only the existing probing, combos, `wall_breaker_combo` and break-bar vulnerability window get past it. We rejected per-type soak numbers on items (one integer per piece and a type list keeps authoring to a few ticks), modelling armor's typed protection as resistance rows on items (resistance lives on conditions and has its own signed scale), and letting every NPC's soak stand against every type (an elemental attack would then never pay off against ordinary foes).
