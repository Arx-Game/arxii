# ADR-5008: A skill is a skill-type Trait

A skill used to be a `Trait` of type SKILL plus a companion `Skill` row holding its tooltip, sheet order, active flag and specializations, with a second rating store (`CharacterSkillValue`, the #4496 "one rating, two stores" contract) and a second development and rust ledger beside the trait's, so nobody could say in one sentence what `Skill` was for and staff authored a skill across two forms. The owner's test settled it: a profile row exists only to extend a row we cannot modify (Evennia's), and `Trait` is ours, so `Skill` was folded into `Trait` and deleted: `Trait` gained `tooltip`, `display_order` and `is_active`, every FK to a skill now points at `Trait` limited to `trait_type=SKILL` and keeps its old name (`skill`, `parent_skill`, `teaching_skill`, `snapshot_skill`), the rating is the trait's `CharacterTraitValue` and the progress its `DevelopmentPoints` row (#5010). The fold was an ADR-0237 restructure, expand, copy and contract in three migrations (`0376` to `0378`), with a copy step that reverses. We rejected renaming `Skill` to a profile model (it keeps the "a skill is this plus that" explanation alive) and keeping the second rating store (two copies drift, #4496). `species.Language` has the same companion shape on LANGUAGE traits and is deliberately untouched.
