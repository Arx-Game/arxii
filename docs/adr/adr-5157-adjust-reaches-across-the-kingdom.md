# ADR-5157: Adjust reaches across the kingdom

The region map's Adjust (`set_footprint(take_from_siblings=True)`) takes a pip from any rung of the carved area's kingdom, not only from beneath its ladder parent. #4988 scoped it to the ladder parent, so a county could take land from a sibling county of its own duchy and nothing else; a cousin county in the next duchy was skipped and named. That made one common edit slow: moving a barony to a coast its county does not reach, when the coast belongs to a county of another duchy, took three border edits (shrink the neighbour, grow the duchy, grow the county) before the seat could be dragged. The map is a work in progress and the owner expects to shuffle baronies, redraw borders and move cities many times (ruling 2026-11-16: borders bend in one stroke, nobody changes liege). Because `AreaFootprintPip` names only a pip's lowest holder and every ancestor's footprint is derived by walking `Area.parent`, moving a pip between two counties moves both duchy lines with it and writes nothing else, so the wider scope needed no new write path; `changed` and undo are as before.

The scope stops at the kingdom (`_adjust_scope`): a stroke never takes another kingdom's land, and a kingdom, or an area with no kingdom above it, keeps the ladder-parent scope, so a kingdom carved with Adjust still takes only from sibling kingdoms under the same empire. A barony's single pip is still never taken; the seat drag is how a barony moves, and it still lands only on a pip the barony's own county holds.

*Rejected:* letting a dragged barony change county, so the barony itself crosses the duchy line. That changes who the barony's liege is as a side effect of a map gesture, which the owner did not want. *Rejected:* no scope at all (take from any holder). A slip of the brush across a kingdom border would then move land between realms, the one border the owner draws by hand and expects to hold.
