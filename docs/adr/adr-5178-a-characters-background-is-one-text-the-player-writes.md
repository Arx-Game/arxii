# ADR-5178: A character's Background is one text the player writes

Character creation asks for the character's history once, in one Background box on Final Touches, and `Profile.background` is that text word for word. The paragraph each life stage ended on (#4872, relabelled "Background Notes" in #5160), the Recent History box under the last stage (#4830) and the lines finalize composed into the Background from the Upbringing answers and the beats (ADR-4124-A: "drafted from the answers and the beats") are removed. The owner ruled it on 2026-11-17 after seeing the per-stage boxes on production and a mockup that kept a text for each stage: "I think multiple separate data fields would be incredibly confusing for players and make CG feel really overwhelming ... We have a single background field at the end someone can put in to describe the background of their character, and if they have personas, then they can put in the background for those characters. No separate fields per stage of life, I think it's just a bad design." Asked whether the composed lines should still be added, the owner chose a Background that holds only what the player wrote. The comparison was Arx 1, where a Background was one public free text (1,998 of them in the 2024 snapshot, a middle length of 242 words) and most players wrote the stages of a life in order with no prompt. We rejected keeping the per-stage texts as headed sections of the Background (the mockup): several boxes for one history is the confusion the owner named. We rejected keeping the composed lines after the player's text: the beats are the owner's and staff's, and a box the player writes needs no draft. A second face still writes its own Background in its door (`OFFICIALLY_DEAD_TEXT_FIELDS`), which is how a told story differs from the true one. Consequences: `draft_data["background"]` with `BACKGROUND_MAX_LENGTH` (4000, sized from the Arx 1 lengths); `draft_data["stage_descriptions"]` and `draft_data["recent_history"]` are refused by the draft serializer and dropped from a saved draft on its next save; `CharacterOriginSlot.life_stage` is removed (0450 deletes the stage rows, which are play state and whose text finalize had already printed into `Profile.background`; 0451 drops the column; the production dump of 2026-11-16 held no such rows); `assemble_origin_prose` stays for the Durance testament; a character whose player wrote nothing has an empty Background.
