# ADR-5124: Fury's anchor person became a kindled resonance, and the bond is one kindling situation among many

> Amended in part by ADR-5327: a kindled deed no longer pays on every authored situation. A situation still kindles a resonance and still sets its strength (the give-in depth, bonus and ease, and the strength an act's mark pays at), but only a chosen-deed row such as a mission option's theme pays at once. Every other row suggests the resonance beside a recorded act, and her player marks it.

Fury, the trade of control for power, was declared against an anchor: a bonded person who had been harmed (`fury_anchor`), and the bond's tier set how deep the character could go (`FuryConfig.provocation_cap_per_tier` and its two sibling fields). Players give in to what they feel, not to a person, and the same cause (an enemy standing before a thread's bond, a feeling carried, someone in trouble, a mission's theme) should also strengthen a pull, a Dig deep and a Rally, and earn the resonance. So the declaration now names a **resonance** (`CommittingDeclaration.fury_resonance`), and a resonance is **kindled** when any of its staff-authored `KindlingSituation` rows holds (a thread anchor present is one row type, and its strength is still the bond's own tier); the strongest situation sets a staff-named `KindledStrengthTier`, and that row carries the give-in depth, bonus and ease that the three FuryConfig fields used to carry (migration 0413 seeded the tiers from them, 0414 dropped the fields and `fury_anchor`). **Rejected:** keeping a per-person anchor beside kindling (two parallel ways to say "what moves her to this", two strength scales for staff to tune, and a second picker on every surface, with Fury working only for the one cause that has a person in it, so a character in the grip of grief or a theme could never give in); and a kindled strength stored on the thread or character (it would go stale the moment the fight or mission changed, so strength is read live from the situation and only a Rally or a GM kindling is stored, as a `Kindling` row that ends with the fight or scene).
