# ADR-4751: The settling rite is the only rite for a changing aura, and corruption falls only through a Sineater

The game had two rites for the story of a character whose aura has grown away from who they are: the settling rite (#5124), where a character reflects on what they did and embraces or disowns it, and the older Rite of Atonement (Scope #7, extended in #1583), which cut corruption at stages 1 and 2 and converted a Celestial character's stray resonance at one global rate (`FallRedemptionConfig.penance_exchange_rate`). The older rite never ran: ritual dispatch calls a SERVICE ritual with `character_sheet=` and `ritual=`, and `perform_atonement_rite` took `performer_sheet=` and `target_sheet=`, so every attempt raised. The owner ruled (2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17) that two parallel systems must not tell one story, so the Rite of Atonement, its Ritual row, its penance rate, the partial path of `convert_resonance`, `GainSource.PENANCE` and `CorruptionRecoverySource.ATONEMENT_RITE` are removed. "Atonement" stays as the plain word for a settling-rite reflection that turns away from something, such as disowning a deed. Corruption a character carries now falls only by daily decay and a Sineater's rescue; the old rite's cut at stages 1 and 2 is not carried into the settling rite, and only Abyssal casts add corruption (Primal's canonical `corruption_coefficient` is 0).

Two numbers replace what the old rite and the single drift tolerance did. Each main affinity sets its own range before Unsettled and its own step width (`Affinity.unsettled_tolerance_percent`, `Affinity.unsettled_step_percent`, null meaning the `DriftConfig` value), so Celestial can be very strict while Primal and Abyssal keep the general value. Embracing a deed pays at the direction rate for the (main affinity, deed affinity) pair, as disowning already did (`embraced_amount`), so a fall toward the Abyss pays and a climb away from it costs whichever choice the player makes in the rite.

**Consequences.** A character at corruption stage 1 or 2 has only daily decay until the Sineating work lands; this was already true in play, because the old rite could not be performed. Celestial strictness is staff tuning on the Affinity page, not code. Production disposition under ADR-0237: the penance rate and the `Ritual` row are deliberate discards (migrations 0448 and 0449); the data migration keeps any ritual row that history or authored configuration still protects, and a `PENANCE` grant or `ATONEMENT_RITE` recovery value found in production is a reason to check before relying on the removal.

**Rejected:** keeping the Rite of Atonement and fixing its dispatch (two rites for one story, and a second conversion rate to keep in step with the per-pair rows); a settling-rite reflection that pays corruption down (the owner ruled corruption falls only through a Sineater); a child table of `DriftConfig` keyed by affinity for the per-affinity range (a model and a join to say what two nullable columns on the affinity already say).
