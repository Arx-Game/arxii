# ADR-4720: A defender resists with what they would bring to a Composure roll

When a social check is graded against a character, the defender's resist (`compute_resist_increment`) is their Composure rating plus effort plus every modifier they carry on a Composure roll: a `poise` modifier, a condition's Composure check modifier, a worn item's. They are gathered through `collect_check_modifiers`, the seam a modifier-aware roll uses, and summed as `extra_modifiers_total` (capability points already ride the rating) under the check lane's taper. Before this the resist was rating plus effort only, so a card granting poise changed nothing in a social scene and a condition that shook a character's composure did not make her easier to sway. The owner chose this over counting Poise alone (2026-11-14: everything on a Composure roll), because one rule covers every surface and a Flustered-style condition would otherwise need wiring of its own. Three things follow and are deliberate. Scene check modifiers and the fashion bonus are left out: the resist is computed with no scene in hand, so it counts what the character carries and not where they stand. A once-only condition counts while held and is never spent by being resisted against, since grading a difficulty is not a roll. A character with no sheet (an ephemeral opponent) carries nothing and resists as before. A staff roll modifier (`rollmod`) does not count: it shifts the dice of a roll its bearer makes, `extra_modifiers_total` leaves it out for that reason, and resisting has no roll. Poise itself reaches rolls of the Composure check type only; Concentration, Magic Defense, the social fatigue check and fury control are separate check types that weigh the Composure stat and are untouched. We rejected Poise-only (above), passing the scene into the resist at every call site for surroundings and fashion (five signatures for a term nobody asked for), and recording the defender's modifiers on the acting player's outcome (it would show one player what another carries).
