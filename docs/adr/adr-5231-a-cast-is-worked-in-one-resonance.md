# ADR-5231: A cast is worked in one resonance, and its power and corruption follow that resonance

A cast split its intensity evenly across every resonance on the Gift's thread, and `_build_resonance_involvements` said in a comment that the split was a stand-in because nothing recorded which resonance a cast used, so a character with a Primal and an Abyssal resonance on one Gift corrupted on every cast whatever she meant by it. The owner and the maintainer ruled on 2026-11-20 that a cast is worked in exactly one resonance, found by `cast_resonance_for` (the one the caster names, else a worn alternate self's, else the leading resonance of the Gift's thread, else, for a caster with no thread on the Gift, the Gift's own first resonance in the order Primal, Celestial, Abyssal so that nobody is corrupted by a default she did not pick), and that the cast's power and its corruption both follow it: the whole intensity goes to that resonance, so a cast worked in a Primal or Celestial resonance adds no corruption, and a pull still corrupts by the resonance that paid for it. Every resonance-tied power term and the place's reaction follow the same one resonance (`cast_working_resonances`: touchstones, resonance standing, the aura's affinity alignment, a resonance-scoped power or effect target, and the place interaction), which falls back to every resonance on the Gift's thread only where no cast resonance exists, so a caster who works a cast in her Primal resonance does not collect power tied to her Abyssal one while taking none of its corruption. The player chooses in a scene and in a fight, so a fight stores the choice on `CombatRoundAction.cast_resonance` and a consent-gated or hostile scene cast awaiting consent stores the named choice on `SceneActionRequest.cast_resonance` (that field means one thing: the resonance the player named while the request is pending); one test, `resonance_on_gift_thread`, decides whether a named resonance is on a thread her forms come from (the Gift's thread, or the role's thread for a technique a covenant role granted), and a resonance the thread has lost since the choice falls back to the default and never refuses a round or aborts a cast. Corruption scales with how Abyssal the caster is as two staff numbers on `CorruptionConfig`, `light_corruption_percent` (10) of the ordinary corruption while her aura's Abyssal share is below `full_corruption_from_share` (50), and all of it from there; the light part reduces the ordinary tick and a pull paid in an Abyssal resonance, never the hasty-fall part, and there is one rounding up after the light part and the leak, so any corruption a cast adds is at least 1. Resonance is earned at the rate of the resonance's own affinity (`grant_resonance` reads `resonance.affinity.earn_rate_percent`, and `drift.earn_rate_percent(sheet)` is gone), so Abyssal resonance grows fast for anyone who earns it, while the learn rate still follows the main affinity. The main affinity stays the largest settled share, and an even split goes to Primal in both `_main_affinity` and `CharacterAura.dominant_affinity`. The list of resonances a cast may be worked in, `cast_resonance_options`, is a separate service from the technique forms, which keep listing only variant forms.

**Consequences.** With the light part at 100 the single rounding still differs from the old two-step rounding in a few cases: an ordinary tick of 1.1 with a 90 percent alternate-self leak rounded up to 2 and then to 2, and now rounds once to 1. The kindled cast power (`KindledStrengthTier.cast_power` times the affinity's `kindled_boost_percent`) always applies when the cast's resonance is kindled, whether or not the cast pulls a thread. Suppressing it for a pull that boosts the cast was tried first and removed: it could not be made exact without tracking which resonance's kindling each pull counted, and as built it dropped the power for a pull that gave the cast nothing (a payer with no perks, a pull paid in an unkindled resonance, an automatic defensive pull that made the result depend on the order attacks resolved). Kindling's levers stack by design (giving in, Dig deep, a pull's kindled boost and the cast power can all feed one cast: with a `cast_power` of 6 at Abyssal's 150 percent the cast term is 9 on top of whatever intensity Fury bought, which is not a double count), and staff tune `cast_power`; the suppression can be restored from history if a kindled cast with a kindled pull proves too strong. Sineating moves corruption in the resonance on the bond's thread in which the Sinner has the most of her own to shed, pays her across the thread's resonances by the amounts imbued, and the Hollow guards any Abyssal resonance on the thread. Creation takes one Primal pick and at most one Celestial or Abyssal pick, which replaces the weighted collection of any number of resonances that #5123 gave creation. The migration adds eight columns and no table (`Resonance.color_hex`, `Affinity.kindled_boost_percent`, `KindledStrengthTier.cast_power`, `DriftConfig.own_side_tolerance_percent`, the two `CorruptionConfig` numbers, `CombatRoundAction.cast_resonance`, `SceneActionRequest.cast_resonance`). Staff must give each resonance a colour (it starts neutral grey), set Abyssal's kindled boost and earn rate, and fill `cast_power` on each kindled strength.

**Rejected:** the even split across the Gift's resonances (it corrupts for a resonance the caster did not mean, and it leaves her no way to reach for the Abyss as a choice); a stepped table for the corruption curve (a gentler climb, but it costs a new table and a second migration, where two numbers cover the warning and the full price); the earner's main affinity setting the earn rate (a mostly Primal character would earn Abyssal resonance at Primal's rate, so the reach for the Abyss would not pay); a main affinity that ignores Primal (most characters would then be Celestial or Abyssal first, so the largest settled share stays, with Primal winning a tie); and folding the cast options into the technique forms (forms list a resonance only where a variant exists and also feed the sheet and two staff builders).
