# ADR-4415-A: A broken hold asks the NPC's cause again, and the cause decides

When a Calm or Charmed hold on an opponent ends (broken by harm, settled by a player, or lapsed), `retest_cause_on_release` (`world/standoffs/services/release.py`) asks the opponent's cause again, after the condition is removed, from both removal paths (`end_allegiance_with_pool` and the lapse sweep's plain removal). An opponent with cause None, or in an encounter that is over, is left alone. If the cause does not hold, the NPC leaves (status FLED, a "<name> leaves." room line): it has no reason to fight. Terms a group accepted meet an Orders, Hunger or Territory cause, so a paid-off group leaves when its hold lapses; Predation and Hatred are not met by terms and are tested on force and on provoking rules. If the cause holds in a standoff, a settled group goes back to OPEN (its `settled_outcome` cleared) and its cause fires the way any cause does: a proposal to the GM where one runs the scene, the fight beginning NPC-initiated where nobody does. If the cause holds once round one has begun, the NPC fights on: the hold was the only thing keeping it out, and no proposal is made, because the fight is already running under the GM's hand. #4091 Decision 21 (a break result never starts a fight) stays true: the settle pool still runs as before, and it is the cause, not the result, that decides. A `FactionRule.cause_effect` of Provoke (which replaced `suppresses_cause`) fires any cause but None, whatever the force, unless every active member is broken; Hatred fires only that way. We rejected leaving a released NPC hostile by default, because the owner's ruling is that hostility depends on whether the reason still holds, not on there having been a fight. We rejected a pending-hostility flag on `CombatOpponent` so a GM confirms each mid-fight release, because it would reach every consumer of effective allegiance (targeting, threat pools, the victory check) for a moment the GM already controls round by round. We rejected restricting Provoke to Hatred, because the approved authoring help reads "they will, whatever the force" for any rule; Hatred is the cause that has no other trigger.
