# ADR-5018: A gift's substance is a room reading every cast of that gift needs

A gift names its substance once, as a mapping onto a room reading that already exists (`GiftSubstance`: shadow is lighting falling, water is wet rising), and every cast of that gift reads it: the room after any call-up adds or takes one ENVIRONMENT power line scaled by the caster's gift thread level, and where the substance is missing (an ordinary room counts as missing) the cast calls it up first. Calling up is priced through the cast's own anima formula: its anima is added to the technique's cost before control is taken off, so the control that makes a cast free covers a small call-up too, and only the share control does not cover spends her gift resonance. The place's affinity (the existing resonance environment) makes calling up cheaper or dearer and keeps its own power and backfire effects; substance and affinity are separate axes, so nothing is counted twice. What is called up is an ordinary source-tagged cascade row everyone in the room reads, lasting while she stays; the room sees an unattributed Narrator line. A gift-holder may also call it up with no technique (in a fight an immediate act once a round, like Dig deep), and a scene's GM may mark it present for the scene; both read the gift, not a capability grant, so no gift is shut out for lack of an authored row. Enclosure now keeps out only the weather's rows (darkness behind walls, rain under a roof), never a room's own readings, so a dark crypt or a flooded cellar counts indoors. Nothing here ever refuses or greys out magic for anima; a call-up past her anima goes through deficit and soulfray. We rejected reading substance from the room's resonances (the owner tied substance to a gift, not a resonance), a new per-room substance store (a second cascade beside the reading cascade, the parallel system the owner ruled out), a per-technique "needs my own substance" restriction (redundant once the gift needs it; only surroundings a technique's gift does not make are a restriction, and those are a heavy roll penalty, never a refusal), and gating the call-up on an authored capability grant.
