# ADR-5066: A movement power is an application of a capability, and each technique use of it is a cast

Combat movement and dodging had drifted off the four layers (properties say how, capabilities say what kind of thing, applications say what it means where, conditions carry passive effects): the capability row itself carried how she moves (`traversal`), how far she dodges (`reaction_reach`) and what it costs (`reaction_cost_kind`), so "Blink" had to be its own capability and a technique that granted it vanished once it had raised the number. We moved those meanings onto `Application` (`way_of_moving`, `dodges`, a nullable `target_property` for "anywhere she moves") and match each capability source against it by the properties the source carries (`source_matches_application`): a technique by its own `Technique.properties` (never its gift's resonances; a gift's substance only pre-ticks a property on a new technique), a condition by its template's and stage's properties, trained ability and distinctions by none. So a `teleport` technique that grants `traversal` blinks and dodges by blinking on any gift, with no blink capability authored. Each use of a technique-granted movement power (an automatic dodge, a blink, a wall phased or climbed, a take-off) is a `use_technique` cast of that technique: its own anima through the deficit and Soulfray path, its own check, its power and the round's related pulls; the roll sets that use's strength, which is the dodge's bonus and, through one shared set of `MovementConfig` bands, how far she lands. Nothing lasts past the use and the standing capability is never raised, so ADR-0248 (a grant means standing possession; a cast does not boost it) stands: a use of an application by a source is the shape it did not cover. Any other source costs physical fatigue in a fight and nothing outside one. We rejected keeping the capability row's settings with a per-source override (two places to say one thing), copying resonance properties onto techniques (resonance is how she feels when she casts, not what the magic does), and a free blink outside a fight (her higher control out of combat already makes most casts free, and intensity brings the cost back).
