# ADR-5019: A blink moves you on your turn by its dodge reach, crossing no edge

A blink power is one technique with two uses, a dodge and a move on the caster's own turn (owner ruling 2026-10-30), so the move half is a new way of moving, `CreatureTraversal.BLINKS`, on the same capability that already dodges: on her turn its holder may move straight to any place her dodge with that capability could land (`reachable_landings(current, capability.reaction_reach)`), crossing no edge, so walls, seals and enemies between do not matter, while landing in held ground still stops her. This is the first way of moving that does not cross an edge, which amends ADR-4663's rule that every move prices an edge; walking wins wherever it reaches, so a blink is used only where a walk is refused. In a fight it costs one hop plus one dodge's cost and is not a reaction; outside a fight it is free, like walking. Lack of anima never greys out or refuses it (owner ruling 2026-11-08: the magic system is about pushing past your limits, which is why Soulfray exists): the anima is charged through the cast's own path (`spend_capability_use`: `deduct_anima`, then `accumulate_soulfray`), and the move list shows her Soulfray stage warning as a cast does, never a block. The automatic dodge still skips when she cannot pay; that is a separate, involuntary path this decision does not change. The reach is not stored twice: one `reaction_reach` governs both uses, so staff set one distance per power. NPCs do not blink (`NPC_TRAVERSAL_CHOICES` excludes it from the bestiary). We rejected a "mode" on the technique row (a reactive half and a turn half), which re-creates the source-based rule ADR-4676 rejected and invites every technique to grow modes, and linking two techniques, which the owner's ruling forbids.

> Status: superseded in part by ADR-5066 (reach follows each use's strength; a technique blink is a cast of it, in a fight or out of one; the automatic dodge overspends into Soulfray behind her answer) · Source: issue #5019, owner answers 2026-11-08; verify against `src/world/areas/positioning/services.py` (`reachable_landings`, `blink_landings`, `check_blink_to_position`), `src/world/areas/positioning/traversal.py` (`blink_capabilities`) and `src/actions/definitions/positioning.py` (`MoveToPositionAction`, `_blink`).
