# 0323 — The Criminal Empire is a recognition won by a weighted vote, not a rung taken

**Status:** accepted (2026-09-29, #4061)

Crime organizations needed ranks the way houses have title tiers. Below the crown the
maintainer ruled that rank is what you hold: a crew holds a corner, a gang a neighborhood, a
family a ward, derived from `Turf` and never authored, so the underworld churns where
nobility deliberately does not. **The top is different.** A Crime King or Queen is a
political fact: recognized by a vote of everyone sheeted in the city's underworld, each at
the weight of the highest seat they personally hold (a crimelord 20, the family proper 3,
a gang's head 5 and its members 2, a crew's head 2 and its members 1; an associate rank in
a bigger organization is not a seat), never summed across memberships and one vote per
character sheet. Whoever holds a strict majority of the city's *criminal* wards (own plus
vassals; lawful wards do not count) may call the vote, during a sitting term too, so a
rival who flips lawful wards to criminal both grows their share and shrinks the incumbent's.
The vote runs a real month so every player can get in, and is tallied only at the close;
a term is three IC years. Recognition creates the tree: every liege-less family in the city
swears to the crown at one authored rate (`FealtyEdge` and its tithe), the deposed family
too, and the crown family holds the city's turf.

**Rejected:** (a) the top tier derived from holdings like the rest (the drama is in the
refusal: a family with the hold but not the votes, or a King whose hold erodes while the
title stands); (b) one vote per ward family, unweighted (invites splitting a family into
puppets); (c) NPCs voting by a stance rule (the maintainer: NPCs abstain unless staff puppet
them); (d) preventing alternate-persona double votes (IC cheating, caught and punished in
character; the persona on the vote is the paper trail); (e) a crown rate per vassal (the
rate would become the bribe and the vote would stop mattering).
