# ADR-4147: An earned display shakes witnesses; ultimates never fail

Power that the table can see should move the enemy, so an earned display takes morale off every enemy who witnesses it, derived from what the character actually did rather than from anything staff tagged. `world/combat/spectacle.py` classifies a play into a `SpectacleKind` (Audere entry, an ultimate, a Crossing, a technique cast at or above an intensity and success-level threshold, or one action that removes a share of the enemy side's health), takes a hit scaled by the caster's level against each witness's level, and records a `SpectacleRecord` so a given caster's move shakes a given opponent only once. Ultimates and Audere pair with it: an ultimate's failed roll is raised to the lowest success and it never mishaps, and Audere and Audere Majora add effective levels to every check, so the thing that looks overwhelming is also reliable. The combat rider is deliberately unguarded, so a defect fails loudly instead of silently dropping displays; every spectacle line, the combat round rider's included, posts through `deliver_spectacle` on `transaction.on_commit(robust=True)`, so a rolled-back action posts nothing and a failure in posting is logged without undoing a commit that already happened; Audere entry and a Crossing additionally defer the morale write itself, through `shake_witnesses`. We rejected authored tags for clever plays, because they make staff predict every play in advance and reward tagging over doing; a GM confirming each shift, and a per-display GM prompt, because they put a stop in the middle of a fight for something that should read as consequence, and a scene with no GM would have no spectacle at all; and a broad `try/except` around the spectacle rider, because it would hide the very bugs the rider exists to surface.
