# ADR-5280: The anima ritual is never performed alone, carries two resonance picks, and its outward part waits on the partner

The anima ritual used to be a solo roll that refilled anima, with an optional partner path (#5181) that also grew one resonance. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-22 that the ritual is a scene with a partner and is never performed alone. Every door (the persona menu, the Rituals page, the ritual's own name, the web dispatch, bare `ritual anima`) refuses it with "Name someone else to take part in your ritual."

The ritual carries two resonance picks on its `RitualCheckConfig`, each from the resonances she holds. `primal_resonance` is a Primal resonance, how the ritual feels to her. `outward_resonance` is a Celestial or Abyssal resonance, the part of the award her partner decides by endorsing. The first setup of the picks is free, and later changes are an attunement.

The award at the roll's tier (`AnimaRitualResonanceAward`) is split between the two picks by the settled shares of their two affinities in her aura. The part of the award paid to the outward pick is the award times the outward affinity's settled share divided by the sum of the Primal and the outward shares, rounded down. The part paid to the Primal pick is the rest, so the two parts always add up to the award. When both shares are 0 the award splits in half, with the outward part rounded down. With one pick only, that pick gets the whole award. A pick whose affinity share is exactly 0, beside one whose share is not, is paid 0.

A Partial Success counts as a success and pays its own tier's anima budget and award (`ritual_succeeded`, the one boundary). A Failure or Critical Failure recovers no anima, eases no Soulfray and grows no Primal resonance. A pick is paid only if the performer holds that resonance now. Held means a `CharacterResonance` row, which persists when her aura share falls, so a pick keeps paying while she holds the resonance, however small its share. The ritual belongs to the account, so another character on the same account may perform it without holding a pick, and that pick is paid nothing. A pick also stays on the ritual after its resonance leaves her held list, and pays nothing until she holds it again or changes the pick.

The part of the award paid to the outward pick depends on the partner, so the partner has a say. A player partner endorses or withholds it. Nothing of that part is granted until they answer, and Endorse grants it once through the resonance ledger. After a failed roll the partner is still asked, and an endorsement grants `AnimaConfig.failed_endorsement_percent` (default 50) of the outward part of the Success tier's award. An NPC partner needs a GM (a `GMProfile` on the player now playing it, or a GM on the scene) and gives the outward part on the roll alone, after a success only. An unanswered question lapses when its scene finishes, including a battle that ends its backing scene, and an answer after the scene is over is refused as not waiting.

An attunement changes the picks after the free setup. Its costs are set on `AnimaConfig`: anima, resonance for each newly picked resonance, action points, and an optional check. It is refused in combat, and before any picks are set (only the free setup sets empty picks). Only the picks it changes must be held and of the right affinity. Action points are spent first and the attunement refuses if she has too few. Anima never refuses an attunement: a shortfall becomes Soulfray, as every other spend of anima does, except when she has no anima record at all. A failed roll still charges the costs and leaves the old picks.

The three migrations are expand, copy and contract: `RitualCheckConfig.resonance` is copied into the Primal or the outward pick by its affinity and then dropped, `SceneActionRequest.snapshot_resonance` keeps its column and now holds the outward pick beside the new `snapshot_primal_resonance`, and `AnimaRitualPerformance` gains `endorsement`, `outward_amount_pending`, `outward_resonance` and `performer_persona`.

**Rejected:** keeping the solo ritual (it refilled anima on a roll with nobody else in the scene, and the ritual's point is the scene with someone); a single resonance with no split between two picks (the aura already says how much of a character is Primal and how much leans outward, so the split costs no new number); a priced hand edit of the picks as in #3029 (an attunement with staff-set costs and a check does the same job and keeps the change something that happens in play); reusing `PoseEndorsement` for the partner's answer (that row settles weekly on a pose, while this answer belongs to one ritual performance and pays at once); granting the outward part on the roll for a player partner (the partner decides whether the ritual took hold of them); and letting a GM decide an NPC partner's answer (the GM only has to be there to write the NPC's reactions, so the roll decides).
