# ADR-5280: The anima ritual is never performed alone, carries two resonance picks, and its outward part waits on the partner

The anima ritual used to be a solo roll that refilled anima, with an optional partner path (#5181) that also grew one resonance. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-22 that the ritual is a scene with a partner and is never performed alone. Every entry point (the persona menu, the Rituals page, telnet `ritual <ritual name>`, the web dispatch, bare `ritual anima`) refuses it with "Name someone else to take part in your ritual."

The ritual carries two resonance picks on its `RitualCheckConfig`, each from the resonances she holds. `primal_resonance` is the pick for one of her Primal resonances, and it receives whatever part of the award the outward pick does not. `outward_resonance` is the pick for one of her Celestial or Abyssal resonances, and it receives the outward part. Three terms are used throughout. The outward pick is the Celestial or Abyssal resonance she chose. The outward part is the amount of the award paid to the outward pick. The outward affinity's settled share is that affinity's share of her aura. The first setup of the picks is free, and later changes are an attunement.

The award at the roll's tier (`AnimaRitualResonanceAward`) is split between the two picks by the settled shares of their two affinities in her aura. The outward part is the award times the outward affinity's settled share divided by the sum of the Primal affinity's settled share and the outward affinity's settled share, rounded down. The part paid to the Primal pick is the rest, so the two parts always add up to the award. When both shares are 0 the award splits in half, with the outward part rounded down. With one pick only, that pick gets the whole award. A pick whose affinity share is exactly 0, beside one whose share is not, is paid 0.

A Partial Success counts as a success and pays its own tier's anima budget and award. `ritual_succeeded` is the one function that decides whether a roll counts as a success. A Failure or Critical Failure recovers no anima, eases no Soulfray and grows no Primal resonance. A pick is paid only if the character performing the ritual holds that resonance. Held means a `CharacterResonance` row, which persists when her aura share falls, so a pick keeps paying while she holds the resonance, however small its share. The ritual belongs to the account, so a part pays nothing when the character performing the account's ritual does not hold that resonance (another character on the same account).

A player partner decides the outward part by endorsing or withholding it. Nothing of that part is granted until they answer, and Endorse grants it once through the resonance ledger. After a failed roll the partner is still asked when that smaller amount is above 0, and an endorsement grants `AnimaConfig.failed_endorsement_percent` (default 50) of the outward part of the Success tier's award. An NPC partner, who needs a GM (a `GMProfile` on the player now playing it, or a GM on the scene), has no endorsement: the roll alone grants the outward part, and only when the roll succeeds. An unanswered question lapses when its scene finishes, including a battle that ends its backing scene, and an answer after the scene is over is refused as not waiting.

An attunement changes the picks after the free setup. Its costs are set on `AnimaConfig`: anima, resonance for each newly picked resonance, action points, and an optional check. It is refused in combat, and before any picks are set (only the free setup sets empty picks). Only the picks it changes must be held and of the right affinity. Action points are spent first and the attunement refuses if she has too few. Anima never refuses an attunement: a shortfall becomes Soulfray, as every other spend of anima does, except when she has no anima record at all. A failed roll still charges the costs and leaves the old picks.

The three migrations are expand, copy and contract: `RitualCheckConfig.resonance` is copied into the Primal or the outward pick by its affinity and then dropped, `SceneActionRequest.snapshot_resonance` keeps its column and now holds the outward pick beside the new `snapshot_primal_resonance`, and `AnimaRitualPerformance` gains `endorsement`, `outward_amount_pending`, `outward_resonance` and `performer_persona`.

**Rejected:** keeping the solo ritual (it refilled anima on a roll with nobody else in the scene, and the ritual's point is the scene with someone); a single resonance with no split between two picks (the aura already says how much of a character is Primal and how much is Celestial or Abyssal, so the split costs no new number); a priced hand edit of the picks as in #3029 (an attunement with staff-set costs and a check does the same job and keeps the change something that happens in play); reusing `PoseEndorsement` for the partner's answer (that row settles weekly on a pose, while this answer belongs to one ritual performance and pays at once); granting the outward part on the roll for a player partner (the partner decides whether the ritual took hold of them); and letting a GM decide an NPC partner's answer (the GM only has to be there to write the NPC's reactions, so the roll decides).
