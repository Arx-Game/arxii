# Combat action economy: one focused + up to two secondary actions

Each Round a PC takes one focused action plus up to two secondary actions (one per non-chosen
category), so the per-round choice is which category to spend focus on rather than a flat budget of
interchangeable actions; we rejected a single flat action count. Focused-vs-secondary is the
per-round decision while category is a fixed trait of the technique — and player surfaces say
"secondary," never "passive."

A PC never acts twice in a Round. No technique, condition, vow or combo grants a second focused
action, haste, or an extra or follow-up hit; we rejected all of these (#4574). Extra actions
multiply damage and stack with each other, so every fight front-loads and the source of extra
actions outweighs every other choice. A strike-shaped payoff goes into the one action's magnitude
or effects, never into a count of hits. Free actions (a mark) and reactions (guards, interposes,
strike-back wards) are unaffected. Enemy multi-attacks (swarms, `actions_per_round`, a boss
phase's `extra_actions`) are out of scope: they are a GM tuning lever on threat, not something a
player builds toward.
