# ADR-4188: What waits on a GM lives on the Dashboard strip, not in the menu

The header's GMing group is short (Dashboard, Story Builder, Tables, Lead GM Queue, and a Staff section holding Story Workload), its trigger carries one count, and the things waiting on a GM (story offers, crossover invites, sheet update requests, assistant claims to approve, the GM's own claims, session requests) are rows on the GM Dashboard's opening "Waiting on you" strip, each a door to its page. Badge and strip read one payload, `GET /api/gm/attention/` (`gm_attention`), so they can never disagree, and a new kind of waiting thing is one strip row from one count, never a header change. We rejected the alternative drawn beside it (Style A, every door in the menu with a per-row count): six GMing rows and nine for staff, four list reads on header mount, World pushed below the fold on a phone, and a header edit for every new kind of thing that waits. The gate is `account.is_gm || is_staff`, nothing wider: an assistant GM holds a GMProfile too, so `is_gm` is the whole GM gate, and staff with no GMProfile see only the Staff section rather than a Dashboard that would refuse them. World is the lore shelf (Codex, Tidings, Journals, Realms); Crossover, which #2446 had sorted there by exclusion and unauthenticated, is reached from the strip.
