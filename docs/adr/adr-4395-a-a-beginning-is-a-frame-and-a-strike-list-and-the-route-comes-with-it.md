# ADR-4395-A: A beginning is a frame and a strike-list, its route comes with it, and the life is picked from grids

A beginning (`Beginnings`) carries the frame every character who starts there shares (`frame`, read at the pick beside its `station`), and strikes whole stages out of the life (`strikes_childhood`/`strikes_youth`/`strikes_adulthood`): a struck stage's beats leave the pool (`offers.beat_pool`), absent rather than greyed, so a Sleeper meets the Glimpse row and "what you carry" alone. The Upbringing pick retired: the beginning's one active `OriginTemplate` is its family route, assigned with the beginning (`services.route_for_beginning`/`assign_route`) and never chosen, and a general write-in question (`questionnaire.is_general_write_in`: asked on every family path, free text, nothing to pick) never shows, because creation asks no free-form lore question (ApostateCD, 2026-10-17). The life is picked from grids by stage: a beat's answers are open while it is pooled and not unknown, a pick is what takes it, and an age-scaled beat (`LifeBeat.picks_scale_with_age`) allows one answer at or under twenty and one more per ten years lived (`offers.age_pick_allowance`; `beat_pick_conflicts` refuses more in the distinctions sync). The people beside the parents are kin rows (`draft_data["kin_rows"]`: a twin, spouses with a fate, children with a fate) that finalize's `_bind_kin_rows` writes into the kinship graph as a sibling sharing the parents, a wedlock union ended by death or divorce, and parentage edges from the character. The owner ruled the shape after the study of sixty-nine Arx 1 lives (2026-10-16/17: grid per stage over prompts with choices; beats are the upbringing; kin in the lineage step; a beginning excludes, never authors). We rejected moving the route's fields (family paths, parentage, prompts, closed distinctions) onto `Beginnings` and deleting `OriginTemplate`: production holds one route per beginning, so the pick never was a choice, and the move would have swept every consumer of the route (the questionnaire, the builder, the family block) for no change a player would see; the retired surface is the pick, and the row stays as the beginning's family-path record. We rejected retiring every `applies_to` ANY prompt: a priced pick-list asked on every path is "lore explained, then multiple choice", the very shape the ruling asks for, and only the free-text write-in is the question it bans. We rejected a per-age-band table for the allowance: a flat one-more-per-decade rule gives the two numbers the success signal names (nineteen is one, fifty is four) and needs no authoring. We rejected keeping an explicit "add this beat" step: the demo's grids are the stage, and a card picked is the beat taken.
