# ADR-5181: A kindled deed pays part at once and sets a share aside to claim

> Superseded by ADR-5327, except for three parts that stand: the Anima ritual with a partner (it earns in full at once as `ANIMA_RITUAL`, moves the partner's fatigue by `Affinity.ritual_partner_fatigue`, and keeps its scene and once-per-scene rules), the per-affinity earn and learn rates with the rate-exempt set, and `Affinity.claim_verb` as authored content (it is now the verb of a mark's private line). Superseded: the instant and set-aside split, the `kindled_unclaimed` counter, claiming, the at-least-1 rule and the claimed-share deed rule. A kindled deed that pays now pays its whole amount at once, and a `RecordedAct` replaces the counter.

> Amended by #5231 (ADR-5231): the earn rate is that of the resonance earned (`grant_resonance` reads `resonance.affinity.earn_rate_percent`), whatever the earner's main affinity; `drift.earn_rate_percent(sheet)` is gone. The learn rate still follows the main affinity. Where the text below says the earn rate is applied by the character's main affinity, read the earned resonance's own affinity.

A kindled deed (#5124) paid its whole resonance on the roll, so nothing rewarded a player for writing the moment up, an Anima ritual restored anima and earned nothing, and no setting could make Abyssal magic grow faster than Celestial, which the design for #4751 requires. The owner ruled on 2026-11-17 that every kindled deed, in fights, missions and social scenes alike, pays an instant share and sets a claim share aside: `KindlingConfig.instant_percent` and `claim_percent` are two game-wide numbers that may sum past 100 (instant 100 and claim 100 is a pure double), and the defaults of 100 and 50 leave today's earning unchanged with the claim as extra. What is set aside never expires. The character collects it with a pose or a journal entry, any or all of it in one pose across every affinity, and the room sees only the pose. It is a counter on `CharacterResonance` (`kindled_unclaimed`) that `award_kindled_deeds` raises and `claim_kindled` grants through `GainSource.KINDLED_CLAIM` and zeroes, not a table of pending deeds, because a claim collects a total and nothing asks which deed a waiting point came from. At least 1 always arrives at once, whatever the instant percentage, so a deed worth at least 1 always leaves the `KINDLED_DEED` grant that lifts a Compulsion when the deed is done, claimed or not. The claimed share is a deed for worship favor and for the offer to settle an aura, but it is not separately reflectable: only the part that arrived at once can be embraced or disowned in the settling rite. The Anima ritual with a partner earns resonance in full at once (`AnimaRitualResonanceAward` by roll, plus the kindled share when its resonance is kindled) through `GainSource.ANIMA_RITUAL`, with nothing set aside because the ritual's pose is already the performance, and it moves the partner's fatigue by the affinity of the ritual's own resonance (`Affinity.ritual_partner_fatigue`: positive takes, negative eases, zero does nothing), not the performer's. Each Affinity also carries an earn rate and a learn rate (`earn_rate_percent`, `learn_rate_percent`) applied by the character's main affinity: `grant_resonance` scales what is earned except for `AFFINITY_RATE_EXEMPT_SOURCES` (resonance that is only moved or converted, and staff and character-creation grants), and `credit_technique_progress` scales training points after the weekly limit and action point cost have counted them unscaled.

**Consequences.** Every caller of `award_kindled_deeds` is unchanged; the split lives in that one function. The "You earned N" line reports what the ledger recorded after the earn rate and says how much more is waiting. A character with no aura earns and learns at 100. Players can now start the ritual with a partner on the web (the persona menu, shown only when the viewer's ritual is set up and not yet performed in the scene) and on telnet (`ritual anima <partner>`); before this no code created the request. Staff must author each Affinity's claiming verb, earn rate, learn rate and partner fatigue, the two percentages and the ritual's award table; none is seeded (#5179). A later design that needs to know which deed a waiting point came from replaces the counter with a table. A ritual that takes in a whole scene can call `apply_ritual_bond` once per member. Production disposition under ADR-0237: schema-only migration, no authored column dropped.

**Added after review (2026-11-17).** The ritual with a partner needs both characters in the scene and in the same place, checked when the request is made and again when it resolves, and refuses two characters one account plays, as Sineating does. Its resonance earns and touches the partner only when the performer already holds it, because the ritual's resonance is player-chosen and would otherwise earn a resonance from nothing; the patch serializer refuses a resonance the character does not hold. Once per scene is re-checked after the `CharacterAnima` row is locked, so two concurrent requests cannot both pay. `DISTINCTION` is exempt from the earn rate, since `distinction_resonance` reconciles against the sum of ledger rows, and every `GainSource` is classified as exempt or scaled by a test. The learn rate never scales a positive award to 0.

**Rejected:** everything waiting for the claim (the owner wanted some resonance at once); claiming only in social scenes (the owner pictured fights and missions first); one pose per affinity (players write long poses, and one pose may claim everything); and a table of pending deeds (nothing asks which deed a waiting point came from).
