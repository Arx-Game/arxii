# ADR-5102-A: A player's own family is built after creation, not in it

Character creation no longer seats the people beside the parents. The Lineage step's Kin section (a twin, a spouse, a child, a betrothed, each with a fate), its draft validation (`_validate_kin_rows`) and finalize's `_bind_kin_rows` are removed, with the `KinRowKind`, `SpouseFate`, `ChildFate`, `TwinFate` and `BetrothedFate` choices they alone used. The named parents and their fates (`ParentFate`, `line_parent_fate`/`other_parent_fate`) stay: they are who raised the character, and the life reads them. The owner ruled it on 2026-11-12 during a walk-through of creation: "Building extended family inside CG is confusing ... Players are apping into a CG, if it's creating a family that should be a post CG activity." This reverses the "kin in the lineage step" part of ADR-4395-A and nothing else in it. We rejected renaming the section ("Your Household") and keeping it: the trouble is the activity's place, not its label. We rejected hiding the section and leaving the validator and seating in place for reuse: a validator for a key no page writes is a surface with no role, and the later feature will call the kinship primitives (`create_person`, `record_union`, `separate_union`, `record_parentage`) directly, which stay. Until that feature exists (#5103) no player can record a spouse or child of their own; the owner accepted that gap. Drafts already carrying `draft_data["kin_rows"]` keep the key, which nothing reads; no migration and no authored content is involved.
