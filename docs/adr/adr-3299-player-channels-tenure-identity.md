# ADR-3299: Player channels are our own OOC models, and a speaker is a roster tenure chosen per channel

The game had inherited Evennia's channels by accident: the stock `@channel` command and its bridge commands on the account cmdset, an unconfigured `DEFAULT_CHANNELS`, and an Evennia `house_<pk>` channel per house from #1884, all keyed to account names. We built our own OOC-only channels instead (`world.channels`: `Channel`, `ChannelMessage`, `ChannelSubscription`, `ChannelBan`) and retired Evennia's: the stock and bridge commands are removed, `DEFAULT_CHANNELS = []`, and migration 0394 deleted the Evennia `Public` and `house_*` rows and their nicks, deliberately discarding old subscriptions and history, so players opt in again; extending Evennia's `ChannelDB` was rejected because its subscriptions, history and display all hang off the account, and per-channel identity, mute, collapse, notification level and order would have lived in Attributes. A speaker is a `RosterTenure` the player chooses per channel (`ChannelSubscription.display_tenure`), rendered as the character name for a first player or "2nd player of Y" otherwise, frozen on each message as `author_tenure`, and never an account name, because an account-wide handle in an org channel would tell members that a non-member's player also holds a member alt. ORG channel eligibility is an active `OrganizationMembership` held by the character's primary persona (never by an established face, whose link to the character a post would reveal) in the org or in a non-covert vassal house (a covert vassal cuts its whole branch), which replaces the #1884 Evennia house channel's synced audience with live access and is a change to built behavior; covert orgs get no channel (a roster would leak membership secrets) and neither do covenant-backed orgs (their members are not `OrganizationMembership` rows). Staff read every non-covert channel for moderation while posting stays identity-gated; a blocked author (an account-level block, or a block on the author's character's primary persona; never one on an established face) shows as an "N hidden" divider and an account-muted author is dropped silently; a muted channel gets no push and no badge; history is capped at `CHANNEL_HISTORY_CAP` by an hourly prune. Web writes go through REST, whose views run the actions with the request's account, because `dispatch_player_action` strips the server-supplied `account` kwarg from client kwargs (`SERVER_SUPPLIED_KWARGS`); live delivery is the `channel_message` and `channel_message_removed` frames, and on the web channel lines are OOC feed lines in the main column under the OOC chip by default (a `channel` feed kind, filterable by the feed chips, posted from a Channels group in the conversation rail), with the `/channels` page holding the controls and a sidebar lane as an opt-in display setting. The owner ruled this on PR #5095 (ApostateCD, 2026-11-12): a second small scroll of text on the side is impossible to sort, and the point of the OOC chip was filtering by tab, so channels default to OOC in the one main window; a side lane may exist but never as the only surface or the default. That replaces the first design, a sidebar mode kept out of the center feed so channels never crowd IC play.
