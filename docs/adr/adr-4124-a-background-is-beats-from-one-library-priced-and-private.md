# A background is beats from one library, priced and never free; the told story is prose per face

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-03, #4124). Related: ADR-0280 (distinctions are offered by chapter), ADR-0278 (rows a parent owns live behind a handler), ADR-0237, #4106 (goals are the owner's), #1270 (a profile per face), #3635 (maturation milestones).

**Decision.** A character's background is written as beats across the stages of a life, from one shared library (`LifeBeat`) that every Beginning offers unless it excludes a beat; an Upbringing keeps only its frame prose, its family paths and its bespoke prompts. A beat is a prompt over distinction offers in a Backgrounds chapter, every answer a priced choice at the distinction's own cost: **a beat never grants a distinction free**. A beat takes one answer or any that apply; "neither" is a beat taken with no pick; "unknown" is a first-class state on every beat. The beats are the true life and belong to the owner and staff (the goals gate, #4106); what the world is told is `Profile.background`, prose per face (#1270), drafted from the answers and the beats and edited freely, with no per-beat true/false flag. A secret may name an unknown beat and fill it in when the subject learns it. A new character arrives with no banked maturation milestones (`maturation_floor`); the in-play birthday schedule is unchanged. An eternal-youth species prices its long life on the purse.

**Why.** The seeded Upbringings asked three free-text questions each and offered nothing, and scaling that shape meant authoring the same questions fifty times. With the Distinctions stage retired (ADR-0280) the personality and history catalogue had no home in CG at all; the beats are that home, authored from the catalogue backward so every row is reachable. Free grants were rejected because they would make every Upbringing a balance problem and every answer a thing to pick for the row rather than for the character (ApostateCD, 2026-10-03); the per-answer scene-prompt was rejected as prompting nobody needed; a per-beat true/false flag was rejected because the lie is the distance between a face's told story and the beats, which only the owner and staff can see. Banked milestones at creation were retired because age should show as beats, not free points, and because eleven points over a life is the in-play curve already.

**Rejected.** Per-Upbringing beats (fifty copies that drift); bundled or carried arrivals on a beat (free rows); a scene-prompt per answer; a true/false flag per beat; a beat count by age (the purse is the governor); a Sleeper-specific stage (one kept beat and unknown elsewhere needs no special machinery); weakening in-play maturation to keep the CG bank.

**Consequences.** `OfferChapter.BACKGROUNDS`; `LifeStage.ADULTHOOD`; `CharacterOriginSlot` names a slot or a beat; the sheet reads its origin rows through a handler; the beat library is content (ApostateCD writes every name and prompt); the coverage matrix is rerun as the catalogue grows. The sheet-wide public/private redesign and the per-viewer sharing grant (#4113) are separate.
