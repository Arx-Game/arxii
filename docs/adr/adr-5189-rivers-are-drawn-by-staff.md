# ADR-5189: Rivers are drawn by staff, and the map file only seeds them

A river on the region map is `TerrainPip.has_river`, and from #4242 it was the generator's: every upload rewrote it on every generator pip from every river path in the file. On the Catenys map that flagged 5,883 of 40,000 pips, about 15%, drawn as one unjoined square per pip, so the rivers read as blue speckle and not as rivers. The owner wants a river to be a thin unbroken line and wants to place rivers by hand (rulings 2026-11-17), starting from a small set: no kingdom's capital should be cut off from water, and beyond that only the long rivers matter.

So the file seeds rivers and staff own them. An upload draws only the major rivers, the `MAJOR_RIVER_COUNT` longest main stems from the file's river records, each as a course one pip wide down the middle of its outline; a file with no river records draws none. Only a region's first upload takes rivers from the file. Every later upload carries each pip's river over by coordinate, so a river drawn or erased with `paint_river` survives. `reset_rivers` is the one deliberate way back to the file: it replaces every river on the region with the major rivers plus, for each inland capital of a kingdom or above, the river within `CAPITAL_RIVER_REACH` pips of it, followed down to its mouth. Terrain is untouched by all of this: the moisture bonus "plus two on a river" still uses every river path in the file.

Rivers do not use `PipSource`. `source` says who last set a pip's terrain, and a re-rasterize keeps a staff pip's terrain whole (ruling 5 on #4242). Tying a river to it would freeze the terrain under every hand-drawn river and would let a terrain paint decide whether a river survives. A river is a line across the ground and is kept or reset on its own.

*Rejected:* a data migration that thins the existing rivers. Choosing which rivers to keep needs the uploaded file parsed and the capitals looked up, which is service code, and a migration runs unattended on every converge; a staff button is deliberate, repeatable after capitals move, and leaves nothing to carry in the schema. *Rejected:* a `river_source` column beside `source`, so an upload could rewrite generator rivers and keep staff ones. It keeps the generator's guess alive under every river staff have not touched, which is the thing the owner asked to stop. *Rejected:* a river as a pip of water. A pip is ten miles across; the owner does not want a river to make its pip water.
