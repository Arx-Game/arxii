# ADR-5177: The Mire is corruption a Sineater carries, held per Sinner, and Sineating is a ritual

Corruption a character carries had no way down except daily decay and a rescue, and the Mire of #5123 was the wrong shape to fix that: it was the Sinner's resonance recorded in the Sineater's aura (`CharacterResonance.mire_lifetime`) and measured against a 30% allowance (`DriftConfig.mire_allowance_percent`), so it did nothing to the Sineater until the allowance was passed and it could not share the Sinner's corruption stages, impulses or limits; and Sineating, a request and an accept that charged the Sineater anima and fatigue, never touched corruption the Sinner already carried. The owner ruled (2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17) that corruption a character carries falls only through a Sineater, so the Mire is now exactly that corruption: it sits on the Sineater's own corruption in the Sinner's resonance (`CharacterResonance.corruption_current`, with its condition and stages), and `MireHolding` (per Sineater-side `CharacterRelationship` and resonance: `amount`) partitions it per Sinner, with the holdings never exceeding the corruption. Sineating is a ritual with a check staff configure, asked for by the Sinner's pose and performed by the Sineater's pose, moving corruption onto the Sineater whatever the roll and scaling the resonance each earns by the roll; it costs no anima and no fatigue. The Sineater settles the Mire through the settling rite, by a reflection on the burden taken from one Sinner and a choice of Absolve, Condemn or Succumb; a Succumb turns it into the Sinner's resonance in the Sineater's own aura and pays the Sinner from a per-tether jackpot of what the Sineater purified for them since the last payout (the jackpot and fall counters are per tether, so they sit on the Sineater's `CharacterRelationship` side as `mire_purified_since_payout` and `mire_succumbed_since_fall`, not on each resonance's holding).

**Consequences.** A Sineater's carried corruption climbs the same stages as a Sinner's and stops at the Compulsion stage (`CorruptionConfig.sineater_max_stage`), where the ritual takes on only what fits and then refuses until the Sineater settles some. A Sineater whose aura is predominantly Abyssal has fallen and serves no one; the fall pays every Sinner they succumbed to. Presence is by character (`shared_active_scene`), not by pairing player accounts, so an NPC a GM runs can be a Sineater. Forming a tether also weaves the Sineater's own thread, so the resistance a Sineater earns finally applies. That thread sits on a fresh ritual capstone of the Sineater's own side each time the tether forms, as the Sinner's does. No tether forms and no plea or ritual runs between two characters one account plays (decided by the loan-aware current player of each sheet), so one person cannot ask as one character and perform as the other. A Sinner sheds only corruption that is their own: a character who is a Sinner on one tether and a Sineater on another cannot pass on the Mire they hold for someone else. What a Sinner learns about a Sineater's succumb or fall payout and about a Sineating's result is a stored narrative message, so an offline Sinner reads it at login. A Compulsion lifts, adds its corruption and sends its line only for a deed the character did themselves (a kindled deed), never for a grant from another player, a GM or a cron, and never for succumbing. Production disposition under ADR-0237: `mire_lifetime` is play state (the lifetime resonance it marked stays on the row as the character's own and only the marker is dropped), and `mire_allowance_percent` and Sineating's cost fields are tuning numbers for behaviour that no longer exists, a deliberate discard; no authored content is dropped.

**Rejected:** keeping the Mire as resonance and giving it effects (it would still be a second corruption-like thing with its own stages and limits, and could not be settled by the same rite or carry the Sinner's impulses); a separate corruption store for carried corruption (every reader of corruption, from the stage conditions to decay and the rescue, would need to learn a second place); and a per-tether column on `CharacterRelationship` instead of a table for the amounts (a Sineater carries a Sinner's corruption in several resonances, each with its own amount; only the jackpot and fall counters, which are one number per tether, live on the relationship).
