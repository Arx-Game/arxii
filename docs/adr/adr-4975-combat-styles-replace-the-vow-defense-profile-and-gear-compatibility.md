# Combat styles replace the vow defense profile and the gear compatibility list

How a character defends (their defense style and how much of their armor counts) and which weapon and armor kinds work with their vow now come from their `CombatStyle` alone: `CovenantRoleDefenseProfile` and `GearArchetypeCompatibility` are dropped (migrations 0365 and 0366). A style is how the character fights, so it is one place to author instead of two tables keyed on the vow, and `CombatStyleGear` plus `compatible_roles` already say which kinds and which vows a style works with. A weapon or armor kind is compatible only when the character's style lists the kind and lists the vow they hold (a sub-role counts by its anchor); clothing, jewelry and every other kind are always compatible, because no style can forbid them (owner ruling 2026-11-02). We rejected keeping the role tables as a fallback for a character with no style: that is a backward-compatibility shim, and no character holds a vow yet, so a fallback would only keep two sources of truth alive. A character with no style has no defense style, all of their armor counts, and no weapon or armor kind is compatible. The cost is that the old rows had to move: migration 0365 carried each distinct profile and compatibility group onto a placeholder style named "Placeholder <Defense> Style <n>", and staff now fill those out with real names, skills, hit stats and teaching traditions. A cap on how much mitigation worn sources give per style is an open idea, not built.
