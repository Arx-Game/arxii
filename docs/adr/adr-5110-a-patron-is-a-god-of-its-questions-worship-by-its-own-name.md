# ADR-5110: A house's patron is a god of its patron question's worship, by the god's own name

A patron question (`HouseCharterQuestion.sets_patron`) names one kind of worship (`patron_tradition`) and its choices are derived, never authored: the active gods of that worship whose Codex page is public (`creator.patron_choices`). A house standing behind a temple is public, organized, political backing, which only fits a god worshipped in the open by that realm's kind of worship: church liturgy for Umbros, with shamanism, occultism, spiritualism and any secret god never qualifying (owner, 2026-11-12). The pick is the god itself: `Organization.patron` and `HouseClaim.patron_being` point straight at `WorshippedBeing`, the house shows its patron by the god's plain name ("let's avoid confusion and have it be the plain name"), and a patron pick travels as `patron_being_id` on the claim, on the name path (`family_patron_being_id`) and on staff's `almanach_edit_house`, never among the option picks. A reader who is not staff is not told a patron whose page has since stopped being public; the house keeps it. This supersedes two parts of ADR-4205: a patron option's `being_nickname` ("the name a house on that option calls it by") and the Org → Nickname → Being direction of `Organization.patron_nickname`, both removed. ADR-4205's other rulings stand: a non-patron option is still a typed row that may be a god or a totem, and the patron is fixed at founding and changed afterwards only by staff. Rejected: keeping hand-typed patron options behind a save-time guard (it keeps a second list of gods to maintain beside the pantheon); the realm's own name for the god, read from a realm marker on the god's nicknames (the owner chose the plain name after being told it reverses ADR-4205); putting the worship on the realm (a realm's patron question already belongs to it through its Family Templates, and a realm with no patron question asks nothing).
