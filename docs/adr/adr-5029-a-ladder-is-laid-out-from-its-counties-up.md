# ADR-5029: A ladder is laid out from its counties up, not split from its kingdom down

The layout tool (#4986) split a drawn kingdom top-down: seeds by farthest-point sampling over all its land, regions grown over connected land, then the same again inside each duchy. On the owner's first production run an 8-pip island off Norbyria's coast became a duchy of its own, the smallest in the game, because the farthest land from every seed is always an offshore island and a region cannot grow across water (#5029). The owner ruled the rule itself: divide the land evenly into counties first, keep whole landmasses where the counts allow, and let duchies be groups of counties, so a duchy can never come out smaller than its county count says and a county or duchy may span water. `partition.py` now tiles even counties over the whole footprint (seeds per landmass in proportion to size, none for a landmass under half a county; growth hops water when walled in) and groups them into duchies by adjacency from the seat's county out, and the walk plans one nested result per kingdom. `capital xW` becomes "the capital duchy takes W times a sibling's counties", since with even counties that is the only lever left for the capital to be largest. Rejected: patching the top-down split to seed only on large landmasses and hop water (it ends the instance, not the class: every level can still hand the next a nonsensical remainder); giving the owner a knob for minimum duchy size (a number for a shape problem).
