# ADR-5292: A Glimpse answer carries its bonus as a card that comes free with it, an answer written in play is final for the player, and no CG points stand in for an unwritten Glimpse

The Glimpse is a monumental event in a character's life, and the owner ruled on 2026-11-22 to 24 that it gives something for free. Each of four parts (Trigger, The Reflection, Tone, Consequence) gives one bonus. The bonus is a card that only the Glimpse gives ("glimpse specific distinctions that provide different various magical bonuses"), and the answer is the choice: there is no second list to pick from. Every part takes one answer, because "the whole multi choice is probably generally bad because players would get overwhlmed by choices". Nothing pays CG points. A character whose Glimpse is not written in character creation (a Sleeper, an amnesiac, a blackout, a Glimpse left for later) starts without those bonuses and "just people get the bonuses when they finally do find out their glimpse and it is defined in play".

**The card rides on the answer as a bundled offer line.** A card reaches a draft through a `DistinctionOffer` line, and a line that arrives *bundled* is held free while its opener is in the draft and leaves when the opener does. A Glimpse line is opened by a `GlimpseTag`. So every line of the Glimpse chapter arrives bundled, set by the model when the line is saved, and the draft side needed no new way for a card to arrive, no pick endpoint and no price rule. An answer holds one active line. A Glimpse card is offered on no other stage, and it sits under answers of one part only, so no character is handed the same card twice and left one bonus short. The model's `clean` holds these rules, the answer's admin inline and the paste table repeat the ones their rows cannot see, and no database constraint enforces one line for each answer: production held answers with two lines when this shipped, and deploy runs `migrate` unattended.

**Not remembering is marked by a card and stored on the character.** A Glimpse line can be opened by a memory state (`DistinctionOffer.glimpse_memory`: blacked out or no memory) in place of an answer, the way an enemy line is opened by a degree. That line carries the card that marks the mystery, so a GM finds the character by the card. `CharacterAura.glimpse_memory` (remembered, blacked out, no memory) stores the state, which is what lets the server refuse a player's write where a story is to settle the Glimpse. A Glimpse left for later is stored as remembered: `glimpse_state` already says it is unwritten. A beginning ticked `no_glimpse_memory` holds its drafts in the no-memory state whatever they are sent. A blackout takes The Reflection and Tone together and leaves the other parts asked. When staff set the state back to remembered the marking card stays, as history.

**An answer written in play is final for the player.** `write_glimpse` is the one way a Glimpse is written on a character in play. It gives the card of every answered part through `grant_distinction`, with the card's secret, its codex entries and the from-Glimpse mark, as the staff sheet editor does. A player gives a card back only through an approved sheet update request (`remove_distinction`), so a changeable answer would stack bonuses: the player may answer a part once, and the sheet's editor saves staged picks on one confirm that says so. Staff may change an answer; the new answer's card is given and the old card stays until staff remove it in the staff sheet editor. The write locks the aura's row, so two requests cannot both answer one part, and an exclusion between cards refuses the whole write. A Tone or Trigger answer shifts the aura the first time its part is answered, in character creation or in play, and never for a changed answer. Every write through the service gives any card an answered part is still owed, which is how a character who answered before a card existed comes to hold it.

The 26 priced pairings under Glimpse answers on production were deleted by a data migration as a deliberate discard under ADR-0237: the owner ruled they were not theirs, and left in place each would have become a free card under its answer.

**Rejected:** free personality cards of about equal price under each answer, also sold on a later stage (the first version of this spec; the owner replaced it: a card bought elsewhere is not the Glimpse's own); CG points for a Glimpse not remembered or left for later (a character would be paid for not deciding, and the bonuses then never arrive); a new arrival kind, "free choice", for a list under each answer (one answer giving one card is a bundled line, which already exists); letting a player change an answer in play by taking the old card back (a card is removed only through an approved request, and the removal would have to be unattended); deriving the memory state from holding the marking card (it would name a card in code, #3676); a database constraint for one line for each answer (it would stop the deploy on a leftover pair).
