# ADR-5308: Every earned gain rolls once per affinity and trickles wait for the anima ritual

> Amended in part by ADR-5327: the paragraph "Only a deed the character chose pays a resonance she does not hold" is now true of every resonance, held or not, and Primal as well: only an authored choice pays at once, and every other gain is a marked act. `KINDLED_CLAIM` is gone, so the claim-exempt line in the bonus-check sets goes with it, and a mark (`MARKED_ACT`) is a rolled source.

Resonance was earned at fixed amounts and spent at fixed worth, so a trained villain and an untrained hero gained and spent Abyssal resonance alike, and the two passive trickles paid out daily with no act or roll behind them. The owner ruled on 2026-11-23 that one skill belongs to each affinity (`Affinity.skill`: Villainy, Heroism, Reflection) and that every earned gain rolls one bonus check per affinity it pays (`grant_resonance`, `services/resonance_checks.py`): the act's own stat replaces the check's stat (`ResonanceGainSourceStat` supplies one for a source that rolled nothing), the affinity's skill is added, and the staff table `ResonanceCheckAward` turns the result into a percentage the gain only ever grows by. Every `GainSource` is exempt (staff and system grants, conversions, a claim of a share that already grew), rolled by `grant_resonance`, or caller-rolled (a kindled deed, an anima ritual and a trickle harvest pay several resonances from one act, so the caller rolls once per affinity and passes each result in); three sets in `constants.py` and a total-classification test enforce it. Spending uses the same roll for worth: a pull scales each resolved effect by its payer affinity's worth (never below `Affinity.spend_worth_floor_percent`) and a kindled cast scales its kindled power the same way, rolled once per cast for non-combat casts. The two trickles now write `TrickleAccrual` rows that move no balance, and an anima ritual, successful or not, harvests every waiting row of the performer as one `TRICKLE_HARVEST` grant per resonance, rolling one check per affinity across its Primal part, its outward part and the harvest; an outward part that waits for the partner's answer is stored as its base amount beside the check result, so the answer grows it exactly once. A pose may name the resonance it reaches for (`PoseResonanceReach`), a deed may earn a Celestial or Abyssal resonance a situation names that the character does not hold, and the entry flourish accepts any resonance, because an affinity skill that only helps a character who already holds the resonance could never unsettle a hero who commits a cruel act.

**Only a deed the character chose pays a resonance she does not hold.** The owner ruled on 2026-11-27, reviewing the built PR: "primal is always a situation you're in, celestial or abyssal is something you're doing to someone else"; "Celestial resonance coming from the gratitude of people you help in specific ways, while Abyssal resonance comes from the emotions of those you make suffer in specific ways. Primal, on the other hand, is what your character is feeling"; and "there needs to be a measure of *intent* to gaining that resonance. Like a paladin hunting a vampire to protect people from it would probably make the vampire feel hunted, but unless he's trying to *deliberately* make the vampire feel terrorized, he could not gain abyssal resonance from it". The game cannot read intent from a state, so intent is a choice the player declares. A character who does not hold a Celestial or Abyssal resonance therefore earns it from a kindled deed only through a row on `CHOSEN_DEED_SITUATIONS` in `services/kindling.py`, which today is `MISSION_THEME`: she took a mission option that carries the theme. Every other row, including "the target is in a state she caused" and "someone in trouble", kindles the resonance only for a character who holds it, and every row on a Primal resonance does the same. Her other two declared ways into a resonance she does not hold are a pose that reaches for it and is endorsed, and the entrance flourish. The first build paid every named resonance to anyone, so a row such as "target at low health" on an Abyssal resonance would have paid it to a hero finishing a wounded enemy, and a trained villain would have collected stray resonances from ordinary acts. Rejected: a per-row switch for staff (a ticked row would still pay a character who had no such intent), and paying on "a state she caused" (the paladin above).

**Deviation recorded.** The spec says a cast never rolls an affinity twice. A combat pull commits at declaration and its cast resolves in a later step, so a combat cast with a pull rolls the pull's worth at declaration and its kindled cast term at resolution: two rolls for one affinity. Persisting the declared worth on `CombatPull` would have removed the second roll, and it needed a column the approved spec does not name. Non-combat casts roll once.

**Rejected:** flat skill bonuses (the owner: "flat bonuses would probably be the wrong shape", so the skill goes into a check and the result sets a percentage); deriving the skill from the character's aura or main affinity (the roll follows the affinity of the resonance being gained or spent, so a hero who does something cruel rolls Villainy and earns less, which is the consequence the design wants); a second roll at claim time (a claim of a kindled deed's set-aside share writes bonus 0 because the deed already rolled and grew both shares, and a stored ritual result is never rolled again); and holding trickles in the kindled set-aside pool (`CharacterResonance.kindled_unclaimed`), because that pool is a counter of one deed kind that a pose or journal claims, whereas a trickle is harvested only by the ritual and its rows carry the facet or room they came from, which `item_meaning` reads for what a worn piece fed its wearer.

**Consequences.** Nothing is seeded: until staff set `ResonanceGainConfig.bonus_check_type`, every gain pays its plain amount and every pull is worth 100 percent, and staff author the three skills and the affinity link, the award rows, the per-source stats, the Abyssal floor and any skill accents. A character who never performs an anima ritual with a partner never harvests, so her trickles wait unspent. `item_meaning` reads accrual rows (harvested or not) plus the `OUTFIT_TRICKLE` ledger grants written before this change; `GainSource.ROOM_RESIDENCE` and `OUTFIT_TRICKLE` are legacy and no longer written. A harvest earns no pledge favor. Production disposition under ADR-0237: one schema-only migration adding columns and tables, nothing dropped, no data carried.
