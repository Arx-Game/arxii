# ADR-5327: Resonance is earned by attested acts, and situations only suggest

A character used to earn a resonance in her aura from any act made while a staff-authored situation tied to that resonance was true, with no reason given (ADR-5124), and collected a set-aside share later with a claim (ADR-5181). The game cannot read why a character did something, and why decides what an act earns: the same taunt is Celestial when it draws a monster off a child and Abyssal when it only causes distress. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-27 that "Intent always matters: that's what determines whether you get it", that "Where I am conflicted is the degree to which we can directly enforce this in an automated way when *intent* matters. Making it self-attestation solves that issue except when players operate in bad faith or in ignorance, and/or a GM also doesn't operate properly", and that "I just want to have a unified system for resonance rewards, if possible, where heroism/villainy/reflection does matter for increasing it". So intent is said by the player and checked by people, never detected by code, and one system pays every gain from play, for Primal, Celestial and Abyssal alike.

**An authored choice pays at once and says so.** A mission option whose theme names a resonance pays it in full on the roll, whether or not the resonance is in her aura (a Primal one she lacks is still never paid), and the preview names it before she picks. The maintainer: "authored content could pay immediately for celestial/abyssal, especially in missions where we say 'X will give Y resonance'. Like we're defining it ahead of time, there's no ambiguity." and "I think we'd always want to be transparent there". Only the chosen-deed situations (`CHOSEN_DEED_SITUATIONS`, today the mission theme) pay at once. Every other situation row only suggests a resonance beside an act and sets the strength it reaches: "we might have situations *suggest* possible resonances to players so they don't forget as a convenience ... Things we can author and point to, even if we don't fully automate it", and "I wouldn't necessarily disagree with being stronger on a circumstance as a -shorthand- ... even if it's not the same thing as deliberately trying to obtain it". A held resonance in her aura is no longer fed by acting alone: "Getting it based on 'is surrounded' doesn't really make sense to me." This generalizes the paragraph of ADR-5308 that said only a deed she chose pays a resonance she lacks: it now holds for every resonance.

**Every other gain from play is a mark.** The game writes down each act a player character takes that has an award row and paid no chosen deed (`RecordedAct`), with the resonances situations suggested for it. Her player marks a pose or a journal entry with a resonance and points it at one of those acts, and it pays at once as `GainSource.MARKED_ACT`: the act's own `KindledDeedAward` for its result tier times the earn share of the strength a situation reached for that resonance (the lowest strength when none suggested it), rounded down, at least 1, grown by the bonus check of the marked resonance's own affinity. The maintainer: "I do like the idea that it would be a self-contained act or point to a game action they took which they're narrating the consequences of", "in theory, any sort of resonance gain comes from -something- that you did, or someone did, or the situation. Like there's some catalyst, right?", and, asked whether to leave journal entries out, "Doesn't that prevent us from unifying the systems?" A mark names any resonance, in her aura or not. She can mark every use, one after another, and a writing carries one mark. A mark is the act row's own marked state (`marked_grant`, `marked_interaction`, `marked_journal_entry`), not a table of its own, because a separate row would repeat the act's identity and say what three nullable columns already say.

**A pose with no act needs another player.** A pose that names a resonance and points at no act pays nothing at once and pays only when another player endorses it, as #5311 built: "pose with no action probably should only pay when endorsed ... it does add a bit more rigor into it. private scenes imply at least two people so they can endorse each other." A pose whose mark already paid cannot also be endorsed.

**A witness's pick waits for the poser, and the menu shows nothing about her aura (the pose payload still carries `endorsable_resonances` for entrance and outfit endorsements).** On a pose that named no resonance, a witness picks from one list of every resonance and the row is saved with `awaiting_acceptance` set: "I'd say a witness could -propose- it. I'd probably have it accepted if the player agrees, otherwise a GM could rule later." The maintainer was unsure whether the game should reveal what is in someone's aura ("I'm unsure if we should spoil whether a resonance is in their aura or not") and ruled on 2026-11-28: "One list, she accepts, but make it easy for an endorser to change when they make a mistake: it'll be pretty common for someone to click on the wrong one imo". So the list is the same for every pose, the witness may change or withdraw the pick until the weekly settlement pays it, a changed pick waits again, and settlement skips a waiting row. The poser, the scene's GM or staff accept or decline. Endorsing a character's entrance or outfit is unchanged and still lists the resonances in her aura.

**Someone pays fatigue for each gain, and a week's marks taper.** The maintainer: "any sort of resonance gain costs -someone- fatigue. For primal, the person getting it, for abyssal their victim, for celestial the caster." One staff-set number on each affinity (`Affinity.gain_fatigue`) is charged to the payer through the fatigue system's own cost (`fatigue_cost_for`), so any fatigue reduction applies once. A payer who is running low pays what they can: the gain shrinks in proportion to the room left (capacity minus current fatigue minus 1, never below 0) and stops at nothing, so nobody is pushed into the Exhausted zone by someone else's gain. Fatigue is charged for what a person causes: a mark, an endorsement, and a tag a GM or staff member writes or confirms; the game's own outcomes (a standoff's ending) charge nobody. A weekly endorsement a worn payer cannot pay stays unsettled and is tried again next week, because the endorser cannot make the same endorsement twice, and a tag a worn payer cannot pay is refused before it is written, so it spends none of the scene's cap. A game-run opponent with no sheet tires nobody: "Monsters probably should get fatigue eventually so we can introduce penalties to them but until and unless they have them it'd just be handwaved. Abyssal is easier to raise because you can take from victims no one cares about. That is fine." For the week: "we may need some sort of cap per week regardless. There's probably a limit to how much you could farm resonance per week." The staff table `WeeklyMarkReturn` turns what her marks paid this game week into a percentage of the next mark; it counts marks only, so authored choices are never reduced.

**Staff and the scene's GM can undo a reward, and what it already set off stays.** The maintainer: "the fact we can't automate it shouldn't let us stop us from requiring people to justify it, ideally in a simple way - they point to an act and if it doesn't make sense, well, we could nullify it and chide them." and "I do think we need to trust people a bit (with a handy dose of 'trust but verify)." `undo_resonance_grant` lowers the balance and the lifetime total by the amount, each stopping at 0, stamps the ledger row with `nullified_at` and `nullified_by` instead of deleting it, recomputes the aura, withdraws a reflection still waiting on it, and tells the player which role undid it. Pledge favor, aura thresholds, achievements, a drift conversion offer, a Compulsion given in to and the payer's fatigue stay. An undone mark leaves its act unmarkable, so it cannot be tried again. Only staff and the scene's actual GM (`Scene.is_gm`, never `is_owner`, since every player present at scene start is a co-owner) may undo, and only the six scene-bound rewards (a mark, a pose endorsement, a tag, an entrance endorsement, an outfit endorsement and an entry flourish) in a scene the GM can read. The undo route answers `{id, nullified_at}` and 404 for anything the requester may not undo, so a refusal never reveals that a reward exists. Private scenes are not audited: "we probably don't want to audit things from private scenes ... we'll just trust they're acting in good faith".

**Claiming is removed outright.** Nothing pools any more, so there is nothing to claim: `claiming.py`, `GainSource.KINDLED_CLAIM`, `CharacterResonance.kindled_unclaimed`, `KindlingConfig.instant_percent` and `claim_percent`, the telnet `pose/claim` and the web claim row are gone, and a deed pays its whole amount at once. The maintainer: "there are no shares already waiting. We have no players." ADR-5181 foresaw this: a design that needs the deed behind a point replaces the counter with a table, and `RecordedAct` is that table.

**Only the scene's GM and staff choose what a tag pays, and nobody tags her own character.** Every player present when a scene starts is one of its owners, so letting owners name a resonance would let any of them pay any resonance to any character, themselves included, outside the weekly table. A scene owner's named resonance counts only when the character already has it, as a tag always did; otherwise it is ignored and the default pays, so a tag never says whether she has it. The service (`create_dramatic_moment_tag`) enforces both rules, so telnet, the web endpoint and a GM confirming a suggestion cannot differ. (Decided in the review of this change; not a quoted ruling.)

**A journal entry whose mark is refused stays posted.** The first design rolled the entry back, but the entry's experience counter, weekly journal count and cached rows had already been written in memory, so a rollback left them true to nothing. The mark's refusal is caught inside the transaction and returned with the entry, as a pose does, and the act stays pointable. (This replaces the spec's "a journal entry rolls back on failure".)

**A pose's chip shows each reader the target under the name that reader knows.** The act's label names the target as the actor knows them, so the game keeps the phrase apart (`RecordedAct.act_phrase`) and builds the chip per reader. A reader who has not seen through a mask reads the mask, the actor reads her own label, the live frame carries the phrase alone, and a target with no sheet keeps its name.

**Rejected:**
- An option on an action such as Intimidate that makes it stronger and automatically pays the resonance. The maintainer: "I'd move away from cruelty as an enhancement ... I don't quite like how it's a parallel implementation when we already have pose-endorsement shapes and claims." It would have made an action better for being cruel and run a second reward system beside the pose and the endorsement.
- A parallel system beside poses and endorsements: the mark rides on the pose or journal entry as a reflection does, and a bare pose still pays through endorsement.
- Keeping the claim for leftover shares: no share is waiting and nothing can raise one.
- A separate table for a mark: the act row carries it, as above.
- A menu of resonances split by what is in her aura: it would tell a witness what a stranger's aura holds, which the maintainer was unsure should be shown, so the one list stays the same for everyone.
- Code that detects cruelty or selflessness: the game cannot read a reason, so a person says it and people check it.

**Consequences.** Nothing is seeded: staff author a `ResonanceGainSourceStat` row for `MARKED_ACT`, each affinity's `gain_fatigue` and `claim_verb` (now the verb of the mark's private line), the `WeeklyMarkReturn` rows and `recorded_act_days`, and they check the reason text on each `KindlingSituation` row, because a player now reads it beside an act as a suggestion. A mark in an ephemeral scene pays nothing, because that scene stores no pose to carry it; she can mark the act later from a journal entry. An unmarked act stays on her list for `KindlingConfig.recorded_act_days` (default 90), and is pruned when she next records an act. A Compulsion lifts, and the corruption it adds is written, when she marks a gain in that resonance in the scene, not when she merely acts. Production disposition under ADR-0237: one schema-only migration; `kindled_unclaimed` is play state and `instant_percent` and `claim_percent` are a deliberate discard of two staff tuning numbers, checked against the production dump before merge. ADR-0041 stands: a mark is attested, not perceived, and the weekly endorsement path is the perceived one.
