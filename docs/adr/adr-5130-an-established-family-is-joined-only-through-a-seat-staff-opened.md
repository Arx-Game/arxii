# ADR-5130: An established family is joined only through a seat staff opened

On the claim path of character creation a family is entered through a seat: a named open seat (an appable `Kinsperson`), one of a counted pool of openings (`KinSlotPool`), or the kin Vacancy that supplies either (ADR-0273). There is no way in without one. The "No specific position" card is gone, `get_lineage_errors` refuses a claimed family with no seat of that family picked (`_get_seat_errors`), a family with no open seat and no opening is not offered in the family list (`Family.has_open_seat`, `open_seat_family_ids`), and the invented-parents card and finalize's `_bind_invented_parents` no longer apply on the claim path: a seat's parents are the ones staff wrote. The owner ruled it on 2026-11-15 while walking creation: "Every family- named seat or one of the counted pool of openings, or the family is not offered. No free option." The older seat browser (#2062) predates ADR-0273 and still let any new character be born into any staff family with parents the player made up, which is the free pick ADR-0273 rejected, reached by a different door. We gave up the easy case, a player joining a large family as an unnamed cousin with no staff work; staff keep it by opening a pool on the family, which also bounds how many may take it. We rejected applying the rule to houses only (a staff-written commoner family is written by staff just the same, and a player who wants their own parents has "Your own family"), and keeping the free card with clearer words (the trouble is the entry, not its label). Whether a picked seat is still open is checked at finalize, not at draft validation, the same choice ADR-0273 made for a Vacancy: a seat lost to another character before approval falls back to finalize's self-serve node and is logged.
