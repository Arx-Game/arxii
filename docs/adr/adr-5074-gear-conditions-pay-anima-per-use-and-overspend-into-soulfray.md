# ADR-5074: A condition worn gear gives pays anima per use and overspends into Soulfray

#5066 ruled that a use of a movement or dodge power that does not come from a technique (a trained ability, a condition, species, a distinction) pays physical fatigue in a fight and nothing outside one (decision 17). Magical gear that puts a condition on its wearer (#5074, `ItemWornCondition`) would have followed that rule, which gives the wearer a cheap power that lasts as long as she keeps the item on. The owner rejected fatigue only for gear on 2026-11-20, and also rejected an item that holds a spell cast on each use, because it needs a new way to cast from an item. A condition an item gives therefore charges the template's `reactive_anima_cost` on every use of its movement and dodge powers, in a fight or out of one. A wearer who cannot pay is never refused: the use overspends through the deficit and Soulfray path a cast takes, unlike a ward, which fizzles. Plain roll and resistance bonuses stay free while worn, like every other piece of gear. A condition with no anima cost set falls back to fatigue. Other decisions made with it: a mantle level's condition works only for a wearer attuned to that level; wearing the item is enough, with no switch; a dispelled or lapsed gear condition stays off until she takes the item off and puts it back on; and an item cannot give a condition a technique also applies.

**Consequences.** Decision 17 now has one exception: a gear-sourced condition with an anima cost. A gear power ignores the dodge plan's "dodge even into Soulfray" box, because a use is never refused. Staff tune a gear power through the condition's values, its `reactive_anima_cost` and mantle levels gated by attunement; a cost of zero is a free power and a staff choice. The reconcile only applies a condition for a reason the caller names, so nothing brings a dispelled condition back by accident.

**Rejected:** fatigue only (a cheap power that lasts as long as the item is worn); an item that holds a spell cast on each use (a new way to cast from an item); a mantle that works for anyone who wears it; a per-item on and off switch; bringing a dispelled condition back at the start of her next turn; and letting an item and a technique give the same condition (every condition ending would have to check her gear).
