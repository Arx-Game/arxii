# ADR-5125: Substances are one shared list, and Primum is the typed essence of each

A substance is a row of its own in one staff-authored list (`Substance`), and everything points at it: a major Gift (`Gift.substance`, null on a minor gift by a database check), the Primum a place or a creature holds, the Primum item template, and the substance an item carries. Primum is always typed, so fire Primum is a different thing from ice Primum and there is no untyped Primum and no Primum number on the character sheet. Ordinary room conditions and enchanted places are one scale by degree: a room whose ordinary condition leans a substance's way reads at the lowest step (Trace), and lairs and creatures hold the large amounts. How substances stand to each other is authored once as a `SubstanceRelation` (related or opposed, one row per pair, never chained) and applies everywhere a substance is used: a matching source gives the caster the full benefit, a related one gives `substance_related_share_pct` of it, an opposed one works against her, and every other substance counts for nothing. The sources of one cast (the place and each equipped item) add into one point total that a staff-authored curve turns into power, so each extra source adds less, up to `substance_power_cap`, and the caster's GIFT thread still scales the result. A source of another substance that the caster has not perceived is never named on the card or the ledger. It reads "Something here works against your ice" or "Something here strengthens your ice". Primum can be harvested only where hostile monsters are (a lair or a slain creature, never an ordinary room condition or a call-up), with a new magical skill and a check, and a slain creature gives up its Primum once in total. Harvested Primum is an item that a craft can consume to make the crafted piece carry the substance. Calling up now raises the place by one step for one shared anima cost, with no resonance charge. This supersedes ADR-5018 where it says that a substance is a per-gift mapping onto a room reading (`GiftSubstance`, `full_at`), that a call-up is priced per measure of that reading and spends gift resonance, that a "weakened by" reading opposes the substance, and that the room sees a per-substance arrival line. It also supersedes ADR-5066-a where it says a gift's substance only pre-ticks a property on a new technique: every technique of a major gift now always carries its substance's property, derived on read. It keeps ADR-5018's other decisions: a call-up pays through the cast's own anima with deficit and soulfray and is never a refusal, enclosure drops only the weather's rows, a scene's GM may mark a substance present, and a gift-holder may call a substance up with no technique. We rejected an untyped Primum currency (it cannot say which Gift it helps, and it invites one number that every system spends), a Primum number on the character sheet (harvested Primum is a material that is traded and crafted, so it is an item), and per-gift relations (two gifts on one substance would carry two copies that drift apart, so the relation belongs to the substance). We also rejected spending Primum to raise a thread or to call up a substance in a cast for alpha, so those uses are `[ABSENT]` by decision.
