# ADR-4145-A: A standoff is the pre-round state of the encounter, derived from open groups

The moment before a fight, when a party can read a band of creatures and talk it down, lives inside `CombatEncounter` as round zero: "in a standoff" is derived (`round_number == 0` and at least one `StandoffGroup` in the OPEN state), never a stored `is_standoff` column, so it cannot disagree with the groups and ending it is one transition (every OPEN group goes FIGHTING and round one begins). Creature motivation is authored once per `CreatureTemplate` (drives, a cause, regard rules) and read through each opponent's template, not stamped onto opponents or modelled as a `ChallengeTemplate` per creature. We rejected a separate container outside `CombatEncounter`, because every consumer (the mission route, the GM begin-round paths, the encounter detail payload, completion as an ordinary VICTORY) already hangs off the encounter and would have needed a second lifecycle to keep in step; and we rejected a `ChallengeTemplate` per creature, because that duplicates the creature catalog and makes staff author each band's motives twice.

Force, which decides whether a Predation cause fires, is judged against the content, not in the abstract: the party's force is scaled by how far its average level stands above or below the mission's level band, and both sides' force follows live state (health, morale), so a standoff cannot be gamed by headcount alone. Tier weights live on `OpponentTierTemplate.force_weight_percent`, next to the rest of a tier's tuning. ADR-0037 is untouched.
