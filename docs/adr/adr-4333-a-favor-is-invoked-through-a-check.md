# ADR-4333-A: Favor with a being is invoked through a check and never spent

A character with favor above zero may call on a being before a check: the system rolls an invocation check whose difficulty favor, Chosen patronage and favor invocation modifiers ease (they add, in points, never below Trivial), and the invocation's outcome tier sets a flat bonus (a `FavorOutcomeAward` row) on the check it was declared on, with a weekly limit per being that a failed invocation also uses. We rejected spending favor, because divine intervention, miracle thresholds, God's Favorite and Chosen anima recovery all read it as standing; an automatic bonus by favor band, because the owner wanted the relationship to matter and the result to vary; a separate pledge currency; a dedicated being-to-distinction link, because the modifier system already carries any source against a being's favor targets (`ModifierTarget.target_being`); a blessing condition as the bonus itself, because conditions do not reach mission checks; and authored invocation text per being, because the system does not define what the being did, only the player's own line does.
