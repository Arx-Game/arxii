# ADR-4333-C: Free text only from a being's steward

A being answers in text written ahead (ADR-4333-B). One exception lets a person write the answer on the spot: when staff assign a being to a GM table through a `BeingSteward`, a closing rite of a pledge to it goes to that table's GM as a `BEING_ANSWER` prompt, and on Success or Critical Success the GM may send their own text instead of one of the being's answers, with at most one clue the being's answers already link. Below Success the GM picks an authored answer at or below the tier, so no fact is handed over that a successful check did not earn. A steward is a Senior GM's table, and never for a being that shows a sign of a god (a public codex entry, a public worship declaration, a Chosen outside the table, a temple), because lore about the gods stays with staff. Dismissing the prompt or leaving it past `DevotionConfig.steward_answer_days` draws an authored answer, so the answer is never dropped and an on-the-spot answer stays the rare case: the table GM writes pledge types and answers ahead on the table page, and players' closing rites trigger them. We rejected free text for any GM, because a scene GM answering a staff god would author lore staff own; and no free text at all, because a table's own spirit would then be no better served than a staff god, with every answer waiting on the table GM to fill the catalogue first.
