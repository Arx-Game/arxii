# ADR-5326: No magical skill is learned by self-study, and a Tradition's teachers decide the skills it teaches

Weekly training in a Magic skill needed no mentor (ADR-4457-A let a self-taught or orphaned character catch up with no Golden Hare and no teacher), so a character could raise Villainy, Heroism, a fighting style's skill or Occult alone, and no Tradition said what it taught. The owner ruled on 2026-11-27: "I never meant for *any* magical skill to be self-study" and "I never intended for fighting styles to be self-study"; teaching Villainy and Heroism belongs to Traditions that are clearly Evil or Good, while a neutral one may teach both and "all would teach Reflection", and a character "could probably learn it from a teacher of another tradition", where the cost is "just that it might be harder". We decided that after creation no Magic-category skill is learned by self-study. A character trains one with a mentor whose active Tradition teaches it and who holds a rating above 0 in it (`world/magic/services/tradition_teaching.py`, `training_verdict`), or through an Academy course whose trainer's Tradition teaches it. Four kinds of skill are governed by a Tradition's list: Reflection (the Primal affinity's skill, taught by every Tradition with no tick, found by the Primal name constant), Heroism and Villainy (taught only when staff tick them on `Tradition.taught_skills`, a many-to-many to `Skill` limited to the Celestial and Abyssal affinities' skills), and a fighting style's skill (taught by the Traditions in `CombatStyle.taught_by`). Every other Magic skill is taught by any Tradition, so for it the rule is "belongs to a Tradition and holds a rating". The Tradition's state (self-taught, teachers gone, living) is never read: the Unbound row is a Tradition like any other. Character creation offers a draft only the affinity skills its own Tradition teaches (`training_skills`, with `required_spend` following the shorter list so a draft can always be finished). An Academy course is refused when staff author it on a trainer role whose Tradition does not teach the skill, and again when a student enrolls (never again once enrolled, so a paid course keeps running). A student whose own Tradition does not teach the skill takes one staff-set amount of extra difficulty on each course session (`TraditionTeachingConfig.other_tradition_course_difficulty`, default 0) and the trainer menu says so in `outsider_line`; weekly mentoring from another Tradition costs nothing extra. A character is never her own mentor, nor another character her player plays (the repo rule that a player never benefits from pairing her own characters), and the weekly run gives a mentor who no longer qualifies one refusal line whatever the reason, so naming a persona reveals nothing about the character behind it. A skill that is both an affinity's and a fighting style's follows the affinity's list. The weekly run asks the same rule again for every standing allocation, skips one that is no longer allowed, spends none of her action points and sends her one line saying why.

**The mentor must agree (ruled 2026-11-28).** Naming a mentor used to need no say from that character, so a player could name any qualified character and train as if she were not alone. The owner was asked whether a mentor must agree and chose "The mentor must agree", and added "The mentor is also not unlikely to be a GM'd NPC, so we need to accomodate that as well". Naming a mentor now only asks her. `TrainingAllocation.mentor_accepted_at` (nullable) stays empty until she agrees, and one rule applies everywhere: a mentor who has not agreed counts as no mentor. A Magic skill with a waiting mentor does not train unless the student holds the self-teaching lever (the weekly run says "{Mentor} has not agreed to teach you yet."), and any other skill trains as self-study without the mentor's bonus. Asking checks nothing about the named character, so the student learns nothing about her Tradition or rating; the student is refused only for no mentor on a Magic skill, for naming herself, and for naming another character her own player plays. The mentor's character is sent one narrative line when asked. The mentor answers from her own account's list (`world/skills/mentoring.py`: `accept_mentoring`, `decline_mentoring`, `stop_mentoring`, `mentoring_for_account`): accepting checks the mentor's own facts with the same rule (`mentor_shortfall`) and tells her which one fails in the second person ("You belong to no Tradition.", "Your Tradition does not teach Villainy.", "You have no rating in Villainy."; the same sentences with the NPC's name for an NPC a game master answers for), since the facts are hers. Declining or stopping clears the mentor and the agreement and tells the student; changing the mentor asks again and changing only the action points keeps the agreement. Who answers is one function (`answerable_allocation_ids`): the account that plays the mentor's character now (the borrower of a lent NPC, else the holder of the tenure, which is how a game master who holds an NPC answers for it), the account that created her roster entry when nobody plays her, and staff; the account that plays the student never. The list belongs to the account, not to one character, and each row names which of the account's characters was asked. Production disposition under ADR-0237: one nullable column on a play-state table (resettable), rows that exist start waiting, no data carried.

**The exception is a lever.** A distinction staff author is the only way past the rule: an ordinary `DistinctionEffect` on the `self_teaching` target of the `self_teaching` category of `ModifierTarget`, read through `lever_total` by `world.mechanics.levers.self_teaching_granted`. It is yes or no (any positive total grants it; a larger value, a higher rank or a second source adds nothing), and the weekly run reads it only for a character with a no-mentor allocation on a Magic skill, through `get_modifier_total` (ADR-0278). The trade: the lever target is found by its two names, spelled once in `world/mechanics/constants.py`, the pattern ruling #3676 discourages and the five older levers and `disguise_sight` already follow (ruled allowed 2026-09-07), and a renamed row grants the exception to no one until its name is restored. In return it needs no migration, an achievement reward or a condition can grant the same permission, and the Distinction Builder's effect form already authors it.

**Rejected:** leaving mentors unasked (the state before 2026-11-28: it let a player name any qualified character, who never knew, and train with the bonus of a teacher who had agreed to nothing); requiring only an existing relationship between the two characters instead of the mentor's say (a tie or a covenant bond says nothing about whether this character will teach this skill to this student now, and it would make who may teach depend on a feature the rule does not otherwise read); refusing the student at asking with the mentor's reason (it would tell her another character's Tradition and rating, so the specific reason goes to the mentor alone); a good-or-evil rating on a Tradition, because nothing in the game says a Tradition is Good or Evil and the owner ruled that staff judge it by ticking the skills; deriving teaching from `Tradition.favored_affinities`, because that field is a fit bonus and ease (it tells who a Tradition suits, never who may teach), and every Tradition teaches Reflection whatever it favors; a checkbox on `Distinction` instead of the lever, which would add a column, edits in `DistinctionAdmin`, the Distinction Builder and its paste importer, and a migration, and could answer from one source only; a penalty on weekly mentoring from another Tradition (`calculate_training_development` could carry a staff-set percentage later; settled with no penalty, so the rule only decides who may train and with whom); one list for affinity skills and styles, because `CombatStyle.taught_by` already says which Traditions teach a style and copying it into a second place would leave staff two lists to keep equal (and `Affinity.clean` and `CombatStyle.clean` already keep a skill out of both).

**Consequences.** Fighting style skills rose only by weekly self-study before this, and no course exists for any of them: until staff give trainer roles a Tradition and author courses, or players find mentors who hold a rating in the skill, nobody can raise a fighting style's skill, Heroism or Villainy by training (learning by use and an XP breakthrough are unchanged). All three seeded Academy trainer roles have no Tradition, and a trainer with no Tradition teaches no governed skill. Allocations saved with no mentor on a Magic skill are skipped at the first weekly run after deploy with the teacher line. Two Required content probes cover the authoring: Heroism and Villainy are each taught by at least one Tradition, and no course sits on a role whose Tradition does not teach its skill. Production disposition under ADR-0237: one schema-only migration (a new many-to-many table, one new column with a neutral default and `TrainingAllocation.mentor_accepted_at`, nullable), nothing dropped, no data carried. Every mentored allocation that exists on deploy starts waiting for its mentor to agree.

**Supersedes in part ADR-4457-A:** its statement that a character who starts without the schooling floor catches up by weekly training with no teacher (and the #4457 ruling behind it, "the Academy is a shortcut and not a gate", for an Unbound character raising a Magic skill by weekly training with no mentor). The rest of ADR-4457-A, itself superseded by ADR-4659-B for the floor, is untouched.
