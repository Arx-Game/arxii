# ADR-5326: No magical skill is learned by self-study, and a Tradition's teachers decide the skills it teaches

Weekly training in a Magic skill needed no mentor (ADR-4457-A let a self-taught or orphaned character catch up with no Golden Hare and no teacher), so a character could raise Villainy, Heroism, a fighting style's skill or Occult alone, and no Tradition said what it taught. The owner ruled on 2026-11-27: "I never meant for *any* magical skill to be self-study" and "I never intended for fighting styles to be self-study"; teaching Villainy and Heroism belongs to Traditions that are clearly Evil or Good, while a neutral one may teach both and "all would teach Reflection", and a character "could probably learn it from a teacher of another tradition", where the cost is "just that it might be harder". We decided that after creation no Magic-category skill is learned by self-study. A character trains one with a mentor whose active Tradition teaches it and who holds a rating above 0 in it (`world/magic/services/tradition_teaching.py`, `training_verdict`), or through an Academy course whose trainer's Tradition teaches it. Four kinds of skill are governed by a Tradition's list: Reflection (the Primal affinity's skill, taught by every Tradition with no tick, found by the Primal name constant), Heroism and Villainy (taught only when staff tick them on `Tradition.taught_skills`, a many-to-many to `Skill` limited to the Celestial and Abyssal affinities' skills), and a fighting style's skill (taught by the Traditions in `CombatStyle.taught_by`). Every other Magic skill is taught by any Tradition, so for it the rule is "belongs to a Tradition and holds a rating". The Tradition's state (self-taught, teachers gone, living) is never read: the Unbound row is a Tradition like any other. Character creation offers a draft only the affinity skills its own Tradition teaches (`training_skills`, with `required_spend` following the shorter list so a draft can always be finished). An Academy course is refused when staff author it on a trainer role whose Tradition does not teach the skill, and again when a student enrolls (never again once enrolled, so a paid course keeps running). A student whose own Tradition does not teach the skill takes one staff-set amount of extra difficulty on each course session (`TraditionTeachingConfig.other_tradition_course_difficulty`, default 0) and the trainer menu says so in `outsider_line`; weekly mentoring from another Tradition costs nothing extra. The weekly run asks the same rule again for every standing allocation, skips one that is no longer allowed, spends none of her action points and sends her one line saying why.

**The exception is a lever.** A distinction staff author is the only way past the rule: an ordinary `DistinctionEffect` on the `self_teaching` target of the `self_teaching` category of `ModifierTarget`, read through `lever_total` by `world.mechanics.levers.self_teaching_granted`. It is yes or no (any positive total grants it; a larger value, a higher rank or a second source adds nothing), and the weekly run reads it only for a character with a no-mentor allocation on a Magic skill, through `get_modifier_total` (ADR-0278). The trade: the lever target is found by its two names, spelled once in `world/mechanics/constants.py`, the pattern ruling #3676 discourages and the five older levers and `disguise_sight` already follow (ruled allowed 2026-09-07), and a renamed row grants the exception to no one until its name is restored. In return it needs no migration, an achievement reward or a condition can grant the same permission, and the Distinction Builder's effect form already authors it.

**Rejected:** a good-or-evil rating on a Tradition, because nothing in the game says a Tradition is Good or Evil and the owner ruled that staff judge it by ticking the skills; deriving teaching from `Tradition.favored_affinities`, because that field is a fit bonus and ease (it tells who a Tradition suits, never who may teach), and every Tradition teaches Reflection whatever it favors; a checkbox on `Distinction` instead of the lever, which would add a column, edits in `DistinctionAdmin`, the Distinction Builder and its paste importer, and a migration, and could answer from one source only; a penalty on weekly mentoring from another Tradition (`calculate_training_development` could carry a staff-set percentage later; settled with no penalty, so the rule only decides who may train and with whom); one list for affinity skills and styles, because `CombatStyle.taught_by` already says which Traditions teach a style and copying it into a second place would leave staff two lists to keep equal (and `Affinity.clean` and `CombatStyle.clean` already keep a skill out of both).

**Consequences.** Fighting style skills rose only by weekly self-study before this, and no course exists for any of them: until staff give trainer roles a Tradition and author courses, or players find mentors who hold a rating in the skill, nobody can raise a fighting style's skill, Heroism or Villainy by training (learning by use and an XP breakthrough are unchanged). All three seeded Academy trainer roles have no Tradition, and a trainer with no Tradition teaches no governed skill. Allocations saved with no mentor on a Magic skill are skipped at the first weekly run after deploy with the teacher line. Two Required content probes cover the authoring: Heroism and Villainy are each taught by at least one Tradition, and no course sits on a role whose Tradition does not teach its skill. Production disposition under ADR-0237: one schema-only migration (a new many-to-many table and one new column with a neutral default), nothing dropped, no data carried.

**Supersedes in part ADR-4457-A:** its statement that a character who starts without the schooling floor catches up by weekly training with no teacher (and the #4457 ruling behind it, "the Academy is a shortcut and not a gate", for an Unbound character raising a Magic skill by weekly training with no mentor). The rest of ADR-4457-A, itself superseded by ADR-4659-B for the floor, is untouched.
