- Comparison notes: Board by board. **Front (screen 1).** The crumb, the one search, the three buttons and the three rolls with a search each are as drawn; a realm row carries its ladder counts, a city row its family, gang and crew-slot counts (singular handled), a commoner family its on-record and appable counts. The Create Family form carries family type, name, realm, the holding seg (Create / Vacant title / None yet), the title tier and name, and under. Two departures from the board's form, both by the code: no seat field, because the seat barony is minted with the chain by `plant_rung` and named from the ladder; no held by on the front page, because a personal holder is chosen from a person's page (Create Family for the person), where it is fixed to that person. One addition: a template row, because `build_family_org` builds an organization from a Family Template (ADR-0268) and the realm may hold several. **Person (screen 2).** The heading reads the tier long after the name, the seg is numbered 0/1/2/3 with lower rungs disabled, Status is Alive / RIP / Missing, the public record is the checkbox and the obituary line. Kin rows are links with gendered words (mother, father, sister), the tier long form, appable, and (RIP) in the muted color with no strikethrough (asserted by computed style). Add kin opens an inline form; with link set to spouse it shows the union kind, the record and the Add spouse people search (capture 02b). The family row and Create Family for the person share one slot: the picker shows Create only when the person has no family, which the fixture here does not exercise; `PersonPage.test.tsx` dispatches it with the person as holder. Titles carries the seat as a sub-line and Add title as a title search. The contents rail has the "also in" group (Roster, Public tree) and the Links rail links the family, the organization, the title and the public tree. **House (screen 3).** The contents rail reads House / Lands (Titles, Domains, Estate) / Books and forces (Books, Openings), the Family chapter adds an open-person link and the tier long form per row plus Add kin under the tree, the Titles chapter carries tithe and Add title · Add vacant seats · Add vassal house, and Books and forces is its own chapter with income per week. The vassals table keeps the existing component's columns (no sworn-to column: every row is sworn to this house). The rail's dashed rows (tier, treasury, pacts on Titles) are the existing component's "no field" placeholders. **Domain (screen 4).** Above, This rung and Vassals in the contents rail; held by with the holder in person and a holder picker (Create Family appears in this slot only when the title is vacant, which the fixture here is not; `TitlePage` renders it for `house_id == null`); Location as realm, tier and seat with no map (the map is #4242); the demesne editable with Save; Holdings with Add holding from the catalog; Books per week; Forces; Vassals with Create vassal domain · Add vacant seats. Departures by ruling: no Post garrison, since `assign_garrison` is gated on an organization leader or steward and a staff-fiat form is not in scope; no Create unit, TehomCD's call (issue Decision 13); no Create seat, since the seat is minted with the chain. **Crime (screen 5).** The house page standing on turf: the Lands group is gone from the rail for a family that holds no title, the Turf group leads, the ward shows its grip and base value, the gangs table has the crews beneath each gang with open and vacant chips, and Add gang / Open crew slot take an area id and room ids until the Atlas link (#4242) gives them a map. The Family and Books chapters are captured (05b, 05c). The board's "kin and associates" is the Family chapter's household band at a rank. **A family with no organization (06).** As the Front and Shape boards describe: people as links, (Missing) in grey, appable, Add kin, Openings, Create organization, no Titles. **Phone (07).** One column, no horizontal overflow (asserted).
